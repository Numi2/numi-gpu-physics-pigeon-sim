# Incoming velocity at fixed supports: spurious reversal

All four preregistered straight-rod controls completed with status 0, but failed the hard-support criteria. With supports starting at their targets and uniform incoming y velocity ±10 mm/s, their final velocities reversed to approximately ∓1.10–1.112 mm/s and positions crossed the target by 1.10–1.112 µm. Both signs and both solver modes reproduce this effect, without gravity, damping, bending stress, contact or actuation. The ratio is consistent with the position stabilization term evaluated on a predicted free displacement; it does not by itself prove the code path or exclude other errors. The displaced-at-rest direction control passed, so the two cases distinguish corrective motion from this spurious reversal. All native states, statuses, inputs and hashes are retained.

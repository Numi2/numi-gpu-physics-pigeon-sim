# Rod position/velocity integration mismatch confirmed

For the preregistered 1 ms coupled fixed-support fixture, maximum `abs(y_final - y_initial - h*v_final)` was 0.00000204 µm with zero damping and 1.467512 µm with damping 100/s. Both runs reported success. The measured dependence agrees with the traced mismatch: the DER stage retains the unconstrained position but publishes its position-derived velocity after exponential damping, while the world stage applies only `h*(v_constrained-v_free)` to that position. This does not prove the mismatch is the only issue; the independently observed support violations, reaction differences and substep instability still require direct tests after a candidate fix. Turning damping off is an experimental control, not a physical fix or a qualified feather model.

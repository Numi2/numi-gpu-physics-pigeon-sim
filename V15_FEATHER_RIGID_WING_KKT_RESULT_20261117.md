# 3D rod and v15 rigid-hand reaction control

The preregistered one-step, linearized native Metal control passed for both node orders and both rest/incoming-velocity starts. It used the current v15 left-hand fixture mass 0.00892542 kg and diagonal inertia approximately (6.19205e-6, 2.72523e-6, 8.82208e-6) kg m², with a synthetic local attachment lever arm (0.008, 0.012, 0.003) m. Across the four cases, maximum generalized equation residual was 4.15e-6 in the corresponding SI units, attachment error 3.69e-12 m, coordinate difference from the FP64 KKT solution 1.80e-9 in the corresponding units, mapped node-order difference 2.13e-10, linear momentum-balance error 1.07e-9 kg m/s, and angular momentum-balance error 2.62e-11 kg m²/s. Equal/opposite force and moment-arm torque are assembled algebraically by the same constraint Jacobian. Complete inputs, device results, hand-property extraction, native source, hashes, commands and independently accumulated residuals are retained under `evidence/crow-feather-rachis-20261116/rigid-wing-kkt/`.

The first NumPy analysis emitted floating-point warnings during matrix multiplication despite finite results. Those warnings are retained in `initial-analysis.stderr`; a second analysis using `math.fsum` for matrix-vector products emitted no warnings and reproduced the residuals. This test models one free rigid hand with infinitesimal rotation and a synthetic point attachment, not its articulated joints, nonlinear rotation, a complete feather, contact, aerodynamics or real flight. The v15 mass/inertia are engineering priors, not direct American-crow measurements. The prototype is isolated and not integrated into MetalWorld.

# Preregistered exploratory wing-drive response under load

The unchanged wing position drive settled short under wind. Test an engineering sensitivity candidate: in the 960-step extended-hold setup, multiply only the four elbow and wrist-flex proportional drive gains by 12 and their damping gains by 3.5, leaving all torque limits, inertia, geometry, targets, airflow, solver and timings unchanged. The values are uncalibrated and do not represent measured crow musculature. Preserve the previous unchanged-gain experiment as the matched reference.

Before running, predict that all 2,880 lane-steps succeed, all four joint positions end within 0.01 rad of their targets, all four speeds end below 0.05 rad/s, and 480 evenly spaced audited poses per lane have no proxy penetration deeper than 0.5 mm. Reject the candidate on any failed condition. Compare maximum joint error and total displacement with the retained baseline; report even if worse. Since the root is free, any difference can involve changes to whole-body motion and the result is not a fixed-load actuator calibration, feather-clearance result, or flight qualification. Do not promote the gains to the model based on this experiment.

# Rear-toe authority and saturation

The matched hard-start zero control terminated at absolute step 257 (5.14 s). Positive rear-toe output terminated at step 256 (5.12 s); negative output at step 183 (3.66 s). The first actor observations matched exactly, and all other output rows were frozen. Positive and negative arms retained bilateral upward impulse for 207/207 and 131/134 first-episode intervals respectively; the opposed-normal proxy lasted 155 and 80 consecutive intervals versus 156 in the zero control. Neither met the registered improvement criterion.

The recorded actuator targets explain the asymmetric action authority. At the first interval, the zero and positive proximal/distal rear targets were effectively at their 1.15/0.8625 rad flexion caps. Over the positive trial, no one of the four target channels differed from zero by more than 0.001 rad at any interval (maximum difference 0.000181 rad), despite an accepted +1 action. The negative arm shifted them by up to 0.25/0.1875 rad and failed markedly earlier. These target and contact logs demonstrate saturation and sensitivity in this reconstructed fixture; they do not prove that increasing joint limits is biologically appropriate or that coordinated movement cannot improve grip.

Both tests use consumed development seed 20370113, an already reconstructed and relocated branch, forced perching phase and scripted carrier. They do not establish a trained recovery, autonomous landing, generalization or biological realism.

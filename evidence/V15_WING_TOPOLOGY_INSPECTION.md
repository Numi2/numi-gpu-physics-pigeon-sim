# v15 wing body and aerodynamics inspection

The independently reconstructed v15 model has one collision body and one aerodynamic wing role per side. `crow_left_wing` and `crow_right_wing` are bodies 5 and 6, each with an approximately 0.035742 kg rigid body and a single collision shape. Three shoulder-chain joints per side provide sweep, flap and pronation; there is no elbow or wrist body. In the admitted model, the left and right flapping-wing aerodynamic roles point respectively to the same indivisible left and right wing bodies, with authored area 0.075 m² per side. The full source-exact model construction, compiled inspector, model identifiers and raw shape, joint, role, and aerodynamic values are retained in the accompanying hash-manifested archive.

A credible physical folding upgrade therefore needs both an articulated, mass/inertia- and collision-consistent wing and a corresponding morph-sensitive aerodynamic treatment. Adding decorative elbow or wrist joints while leaving all wing mass, collision geometry and air forces on the old body would not meet that requirement. The model revision would alter state topology and invalidate v15 checkpoint, actuator, policy and flight-qualification identities; it requires an explicit state-transition protocol and a fresh flight qualification. The companion corvid measurement archives provide joint-pair and geometry constraints, but do not supply specimen-specific full muscle and feather mass distributions or crow flight forces. No new physical folding model is claimed by this inspection.

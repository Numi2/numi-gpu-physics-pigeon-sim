# Lowered wing and fixed hip screen

On the consumed hard-start reconstructed-perch fixture, both arms used the -0.4-radian physical wing target with identical support/tilt gating. With fixed actor bias -0.90 on both hip slots, the simulation completed 500 steps without termination and peak tilt was 0.186445 radians; both feet had net upward branch impulse and the front/rear toe-normal opposition proxy was present in all 500 intervals. This meets the preregistered short hard-start screening gate. With hip bias -1.00, the first termination occurred at interval 430 and the toe-opposition proxy persisted for 385 of those intervals. It fails the same gate. The -0.75 hip arm terminated at interval 454 in the matched wing experiment.

The -0.90 setting is a fixed prescribed output, not a learned policy. The toe proxy does not prove grasp, the fixture is not an autonomous landing, and v15 has no articulated wrist or elbow for physical feather folding. The longer and easy-start tests were preregistered separately before the second arm's result was examined. All checkpoints, model hashes, commands, action-pack tests, trajectories and 16-substep contact records are preserved in the adjacent archive.

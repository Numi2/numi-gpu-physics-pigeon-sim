These boxes approximate the feather envelope and are a poor rigid-bone model. They also yielded high corrective speeds in the one-microstep probe. No physical fold or flight claim is warranted.

Try an explicit, uncalibrated bone-proxy alternative: replace only the forearm and hand boxes with local-y capsules of 3 mm radius. Thus the proxies leave about 14 mm across the wrist while preserving mass, inertia and anchors. This is a coarse collision proxy, not anatomical measurement or feather contact; no pair is excluded and all previous tests/evidence remain.

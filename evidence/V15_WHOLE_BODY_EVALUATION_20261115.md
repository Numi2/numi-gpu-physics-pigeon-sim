# Whole-body balance: evaluation and video

The v3 experiment exposes bounded flap, wing sweep and pronation, tail, knees, neck and hip-roll residual targets to the existing perching policy, alongside its existing hip, ankle and grouped toe controls. Joint limits and native contact apply; some body channels are coupled. This is a research fixture, not a complete independent whole-body controller. A mapped neck/beak overlap found during the preceding v2 probe was corrected and verified before training. Full source, checkpoints, observations, actions, substep contact logs, rejected controls and exact inputs are retained in the archives.

Four PPO updates changed only the ten newly enabled output rows, using separately clipped actor and critic gradients and post-final-update per-channel KL. On retained observations the old eight deterministic outputs are identical; new target movements were small. Identical initial-state authority ablation on development seed 20370113 terminated at absolute step 258 with the new controls versus step 256 without them, a 0.04-second difference, below the preregistered improvement threshold. Both feet had contact and net upward impulse throughout these first episodes; the recorded opposed front/rear normal pattern is only a geometric contact proxy, not proof of a secure grip. On development seed 20370127, the four-update policy did not terminate in 400 resumed intervals. These results do not qualify balance.

GAE versus discounted Monte Carlo advantage correlation was 0.9968–0.9991 across four exploratory first episodes. The critic predicted a more negative value than the realized terminal reward, making the measured terminal advantages positive. Neither correlation nor this sign alone establishes the main cause of failure.

The 73.16-second `crow-v15-wholebody-five-angle-20261115.mp4` shows the old separate 48-second collision-disabled flight replay, the actual four-update seed-20370113 first episode through its 5.16-second termination, and an earlier different-policy seed-20370127 hold. Five synchronized cameras show front, back, above, below and hip/feet close-up; the ground is visually hidden only in the below view. The branch was relocated, the perching phase was forced and a scripted carrier remains active. The scene does not demonstrate an autonomous landing, an exact historical checkpoint, unused-layout qualification, robust grip, or generalization. The earlier hold's toe causation was not established by ablation. Neither development seed is held out.

The matched fixed-rate test and its physical traces are reported separately. Archive parts are ordered by their `.partNN` suffix; the manifest contains the reconstructed archive hash and SHA-256 of each original file. Full restore was verified locally before publishing.

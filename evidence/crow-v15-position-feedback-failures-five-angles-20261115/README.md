# Two failed perch-feedback trials from five angles

The combined 50 fps video shows the positive position-feedback trial followed by the negative one, with synchronized front, back, above, below and hip/foot views. It uses recorded native trial body states from the retained `crow-v15-perch-position-feedback-20261115.tar.gz` archive. Relative control frames 551–678 and 551–697 are emitted respectively, stopping at the first terminal frame. Five unrelated original frames are used only as non-emitted rendering pre-roll; this is not a continuous flight or landing recording. The branch was relocated during construction of the reconstructed perch checkpoint. The renderer's feather geometry is an estimate and the wings remain spread.

Both trials failed to recover after an internally commanded hip perturbation. The positive trial terminated at step 678 and the negative at step 697, versus 693 for the pulse-only control. The individual MP4s are the five renderer outputs for each trial, and their bytes were independently compared by SHA-256 against the remote machine before composing the video. The `video-sha256.json` records local output hashes. `render.sh`, `splice.swift`, and `make_video.py` record the rendering and layout procedure; renderer identity and changes are retained in the earlier `crow-latest-perch-renderer-identity-and-diff-20261115.txt` evidence. This video is a visualization of a failed research fixture, not proof of neural recovery or autonomous landing.

# Retained arrival rollout: advantage horizon diagnostic

The 4-lane, 512-step arrival training rollout contains nine done events, which include repeated episodes after resets. At 100 control intervals (2 seconds) before these terminations, the configured gamma 0.99 and lambda 0.995 yields mean advantage +0.226; a counterfactual gamma 0.999, lambda 0.995 gives mean -0.869 and opposite sign for all nine samples. At 150 intervals the sign differs for two of nine. The terminal TD-error coefficient at 100 intervals is 0.222 versus 0.548. A same-gamma lambda-1 estimate differs in sign for three of nine at 100 intervals. Exact samples and rollout hash are in the JSON.

These are counterfactual GAE calculations on the same retained behavior and the same imperfect old critic, with repeated samples and different return objectives. They do not establish true advantage, unbiased long returns, or policy improvement. They do suggest testing whether longer attribution helps train corrections before the observed fall; compare physical first-episode outcomes against the existing baseline rather than selecting by KL or training reward. The original rollout and failed candidate are archived separately.

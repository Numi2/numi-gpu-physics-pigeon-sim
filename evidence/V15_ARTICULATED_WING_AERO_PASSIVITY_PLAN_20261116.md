# Passive articulated-wing aerodynamics energy screen

The six-link quasi-steady load callback passed the previously registered one-step force/control comparison, with no wake, feather shielding, body drag, tail drag, gravity, contact, policy, or unsteady lift. Determine whether those same loads remain dissipative under continuous passive motion. This is an exploratory numerical validity screen, not a claim of crow flight.

Use three simultaneous v3 environments from default q: lane 0 starts at rest; lane 1 has root linear velocity (+5,0,0) m/s; lane 2 has the same root velocity and both elbow rates +0.2 and both wrist rates -0.1 rad/s. All have zero gravity, zero wind, zero efforts and disabled body damping. Run two matched continuous 240-step one-second trajectories at 240 Hz with four substeps: the native six-link aero callback disabled versus enabled. No resets, task, teacher or learned policy. Use the same 24 blade-element surface/closure parameters as the one-step experiment. Retain initial q/v, all q/v observations, per-step native status, parameters and build identities. Independently compute physical kinetic energy for every accepted state using the FP64 articulated invariants calculation, including rotor armature.

Prediction: all 1440 lane-steps (two arms × three lanes × 240 steps) are accepted and finite; lane 0 remains exactly still in both arms; lane 1 and lane 2 each lose at least 0.001 J with aero on; their no-aero final energies differ from initial by less than 0.005 J; no individual aero-on control-step energy increase exceeds 0.0001 J. A failure to calculate any energy or any failed solver step fails the screen. Report all three energy curves and worst increases, not just terminal values. Stop after one matched batch and preserve contradictions. Airfoil coefficients, segment geometry, physical bird mass/feather distribution, lift transients, collisions and real-world flight are not validated by a pass.

# Joint whole-body policy training after toe saturation screen

The v3 actor's newly exposed wing/tail/knee/neck/hip-roll rows were trained with leg and toe outputs frozen; fixed-wing and fixed-tail perturbations did not recover the hard start. Rear-toe opening materially hastened failure, while closing harder barely changed the four target positions because they were already at the established flexion cap. This supports testing coordinated leg, ankle and toe adjustments with the other body controls; it does not establish the cause of the fall or justify exceeding joint limits.

Make an isolated copy of the previously validated full-18-action PPO worker, changing only the selected residual output rows from 8–17 to 0–17. The 42-unit existing body/contact feature path and all 18 action rows may be trained; inherited actor trunk, output connections outside the 42-column residual tail, every bias, observation normalizer, exploration scale and semantic contract stay frozen. Critic gradients remain separately clipped. Start from the identical v3 initial stochastic policy and fresh optimizer; run four 256-step one-lane updates, alternating physically reached consumed hard step-150 and easy step-50 checkpoints, same seeds and seed order, two epochs, fixed rate 0.0003, gamma 0.999 and lambda 0.995. Use only first-episode samples for PPO, retaining all post-fall data. Verify full-18-action native behavior log probability, all protected weights/optimizer moments, finite metrics and final-step per-channel KL after each update. The first collected rollout must match the earlier v3 update-01 byte-for-byte; if it does not, stop without comparison. No adaptive learning rate.

Test the four-update deterministic candidate with teacher off and no scheduled resets from the same v3 step-49 hard and step-50 easy physical checkpoints, 400 intervals each, with full contact logs and first-episode analysis. Exploratory promotion requires the hard start to first terminate at least 10 intervals later than its zero baseline absolute step 257, and the easy start to remain nonterminated with maximum tilt at most 0.15 rad over the first 250 resumed intervals. On both, record bilateral upward contact and opposed toe-normal proxy, distinguishing that proxy from validated grip. Screen failures, optimizer states, full actions, source, logs and checkpoints are retained. These are consumed development conditions on a relocated, reconstructed branch, forced phase and scripted carrier: no generalization or autonomous landing. If the candidate meets the screen, preregister a longer, stricter and previously unused disturbance evaluation before any mission claim.

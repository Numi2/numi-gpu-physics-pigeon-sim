# Reconstructed perch action-history and phase controls

The initial 72-float restore attempt did not execute because its guard incorrectly counted a separate raw-action area as a fourth history slot. Its traces and code are archived. The corrected 54-float action-history restore did execute, but its first knee target remained 0.280 rad and six first-substep leg efforts saturated at +/-0.75 and +/-0.60. On development seeds 20310111 and 20310129, both corrected-history trials first terminated at interval 229 for tilt, with 151 consecutive qualifying intervals. Original exact-q/v-only controls had first done at 230 and 231, respectively. Thus the action history by itself did not remove the kick or materially improve these trials.

Adding the phase field initialized to 1.0 according to the existing elapsed-journey formula changed the first knee target to 0.415 rad, matching the uninterrupted source, and the six first-substep leg efforts became approximately -0.024, -0.002, 0.036, -0.023, 0.013 and 0.048 rather than saturated. In the phase-plus-history trials, seed 20310111 held the strict support/grip/tilt/speed gates for 372 successive intervals and first terminated for tilt at 450; seed 20310129 completed all 500 intervals with all gates satisfied and no done. These are two used development seeds on a relocated-branch, scripted-carrier, partial q/v reconstruction, not a restored full checkpoint, an autonomous landing or a generalization result.

All four history-only control traces and both phase trials are stored in per-run `crow-settled-...tar.gz` archives with per-file verified manifests. Generated shader and observer sources, compile metadata and shader binaries are in `crow-phase-reset-builds-20261115.tar.gz`, SHA-256 `a6c22d672b332b12bbb933a376aa873574cd36a1f0c2348c3519f9cc665c54f3`.

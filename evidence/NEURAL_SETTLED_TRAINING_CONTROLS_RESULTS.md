# Matched phase-corrected stochastic controls

On the same used seed and four partial-reconstruction lanes, the baseline (-3.5 balance-action log standard deviation) first terminated for tilt at 146, 149, 146 and 142 intervals, with 12 total done events. With first-four balance noise reduced to -5, the first terminations for tilt were 197, 208, 220 and 209, with eight total done events. Thus reducing this exploration extended survival but did not yield an episode longer than 4.4 seconds.

The terminal-carrier alignment variant generated bit-for-bit identical transitions and actor observations to the -3.5 baseline, including all 12 done events and the same output policy and learner state. These particular branch changes did not affect this rollout. The maximum behavior log-probability error was 9.54e-6 for baseline and alignment, and 2.29e-5 for minimum noise. The respective final combined balance KLs after the diagnostic updates were 0.0347, 0.0347 and 70.04. No generated update was promoted. Full rollouts, model packs, learner states, shader sources/binaries, logs and per-file hashes are preserved in the `crow-settled-training-...` archives. These used-seed fixture experiments are not full autonomous missions.

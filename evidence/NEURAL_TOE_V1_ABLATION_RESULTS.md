# Toe-output ablation of the eight-action update

A deterministic ablation zeroed only the four output rows for the new toe residuals; the updated actor trunk and leg output rows were identical. On the consumed regression seed 20310129, the full policy fell at 307 intervals with a longest strict streak of 187; the zero-toe policy had no done by 1000 and a longest streak of 960. The first support/contact gate disagreement arose at interval 36, with tilt separation exceeding 0.01 rad at 91. This supports a causal effect of the toe outputs on this matched simulated start, without establishing biological realism or excluding contact-solver sensitivity.

On consumed seed 20310201, both full and zero-toe variants had no done by 1000 and each had a 961-interval longest strict streak. Thus this outcome does not establish that the learned toe residuals caused that start's improvement versus the older leg policy. The updated leg policy with zero toe residual has only these two ablation tests; it is not qualified for other starts or independent layouts. The scripted carrier still closes toes in both arms. Raw full-substep traces, first episodes, post-failure resets, policies and analysis are archived; resets never count toward hold time.

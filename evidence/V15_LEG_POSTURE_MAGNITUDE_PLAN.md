# Negative hip-posture magnitude follow-up

The preregistered -0.5 bilateral hip action offset extended the hard-seed first episode from 208 to 275 intervals and increased the opposed-normal proxy streak from 156 to 226, but still fell. Positive +0.5 fell sooner. Test whether the effect has a useful local range rather than assume that more or less offset is better.

Using the same deterministic source actor, v3 step-49 checkpoint, v3 shader and control settings, run three new packs with identical offsets to hip output slots 0 and 1: -0.25, -0.75 and -1.0. Everything else stays fixed, actor action and physical target clamps remain active, and all accepted targets and 16-substep contacts are recorded. Run 500 continuation intervals (10 s), no teacher or scheduled resets; count only the first episode. All variants must start with the same actor observation as the archived zero control. Their physical reference is the preceding -0.5 trial, including its first termination (relative 275) and 226-interval longest bilateral opposed-normal proxy; this is exploratory selection on an already-consumed hard seed.

A candidate can advance to an independently planned easy-start test only if it survives at least 10 further intervals beyond 275 and increases the opposed-normal proxy streak by at least 10 beyond 226, while retaining bilateral positive upward impulse through the first episode. A stronger command that improves one measure while losing support is rejected. Stop after the three declared arms; do not extend the range or tune until a passing trial appears. A successful fixed-offset screen is not a neural policy, genuine grasp validation, autonomous landing or biological realism. Preserve every control, intervention, outcome and failure. The existing source branch, forced phase, relocated reconstructed fixture and scripted carrier remain explicit limitations; unused seeds/layouts remain unexposed.

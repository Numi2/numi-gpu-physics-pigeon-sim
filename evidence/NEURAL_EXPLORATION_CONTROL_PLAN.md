This is a physical fall in that probe, not a reason to disable the termination. Training samples untrained head/beak actions 15–17 as well as the four trained residuals. The latter affect physical actuation; their unchanged Gaussian likelihood factors cancel from the masked PPO ratio, but their physical noise is a confound.

Conditions: unchanged baseline; lower first-four balance log std to -3.5; lower 15–17 head/beak log std to -3.5; lower both groups to -3.5. Other outputs keep their original std. Verify exact weight and contract equality after pack serialization. Each uses four environments, 512 steps, identical seed 20270511, the same v4 randomized starts, signed external push curriculum and simulator, and a fresh learner optimizer; collect stochastic rollout first and retain the subsequent diagnostic learner update without using it as a candidate. These controls do not establish real-world robustness or causal sensory feedback; evaluate any later trained policy independently.

# Live articulated wing kinematics instrument: pass at three declared states

The native 41-body fixture ran three one-step free-motion environments without a failed step. A read-only pre-dynamics device callback copied all body states before actuation; its capture was checked against independent FP64 COM kinematics for the exact same q/v. Max absolute component errors across all bodies and lanes were 5.44e-8 m for position, 7.10e-8 for quaternion components up to sign, 4.19e-8 m/s for linear velocity and 3.51e-8 rad/s for angular velocity, below the preregistered 1e-5 limits. The zero-rate lane stayed motionless; the elbow-only lane moved its left hand while the right hand stayed still; the bilateral/root-motion lane had nonzero motion in both hands.

This supports using the borrowed pre-dynamics body-state stream for the next isolated aerodynamic force calculation on this exact topology and configuration. It does not prove air-force accuracy, contact behavior, different devices or all possible poses. Raw output, source, commands and input hashes are retained under `crow-v15-wing-continuation-20261116/wing-live-kinematics-20261116/`. Prior reports of zero link velocities are not evidence against this tested callback path, nor does this test establish other publication paths.

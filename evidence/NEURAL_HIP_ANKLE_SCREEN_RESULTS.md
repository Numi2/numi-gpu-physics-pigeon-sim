# Paired hip and ankle feedback screen

The 16-lane exploratory screen was repeated with the same seed, policy, per-lane gain grid and fixture; its only shader change applied the bounded pitch/rate feedback to the two ankle drives instead of the two hip drives. All 16 lanes' actor observations were byte-identical for the first 40 steps, and the unchanged control lane's observations matched for all 1000 steps. Every modified lane first differed at observation index 51. This supports matching the pre-intervention trajectories; it does not guarantee identical unobserved state beyond the checked observation contract.

Among the 15 feedback pairs, the ankle trial terminated later in 10, the hip trial later in four, and one pair remained unterminated at the 1000-interval horizon. For lane 4, hip feedback terminated on contact at interval 179 while ankle feedback terminated on tilt at 497. These are first-episode termination times, not strict substep grip measurements. Gains differ among lanes, so the counts do not prove a universally better joint or establish generalization. Lane 4's ankle gains (-0.06 pitch, +0.06 pitch rate, 0.04-radian bound) were selected for matched single-start physical testing. All rollouts and source are retained. The controller is privileged and the fixture reconstructed with a scripted carrier.

# Correction: sampled head and beak nuisance actions

Earlier plans described the branch learner as executing only four residual outputs. That is incomplete during stochastic training. The v15 action kernel routes slots 15–17 (head yaw, head pitch and beak) to their position actuators even though the PPO objective and recorded final KL use slots 0–3 only. The retained v3 update-08 rollout has nuisance latent standard deviations [0.1352, 0.1360, 0.1367], so these actions were sampled and can affect physical training. Deterministic evaluation replays record zero for these three actions. The other actor outputs are overridden by the scripted terminal carrier during the forced phase.

Across all 16 saved v1/v2/v3 update policy packs, the output weight rows and biases for slots 15–17 are exactly zero and their log standard deviations remain exactly -2.0. Thus their independent Gaussian conditional distribution is unchanged by the recorded PPO updates, and its likelihood factor cancels between behavior and updated policies; the four-slot importance ratio is valid for that fixed nuisance distribution. This conclusion depends on the zero rows and fixed log standard deviations, and must be rechecked if architecture, optimizer or initialization changes. The sampled nuisance actions nevertheless introduce training noise, and the outcomes are not evidence that head/beak behavior was trained.

Scripts and machine-readable results in `audit_neural_nuisance.py`, `audit_all_neural_nuisance.py`, `neural-nuisance-audit.json` and `neural-all-nuisance-audit.json` preserve this check. The raw native likelihood parity diagnostics check all 18 sampled dimensions. Future standalone balance training should explicitly immobilize or include any other physically executed stochastic output rather than assume the four-slot contract.

# Position-only COM/contact feedback: no recovery

Two signs of bounded bilateral hip feedback were applied after the same internal joint perturbation on the consumed hard-start reconstructed branch fixture, with the lowered wing posture and fixed -0.90 hip bias. Their initial 49-frame checkpoint prefixes matched the reference in q, v, actor observations, actions, body states, reward and outcome; through the first changed target they matched the positive-pulse-only control in q, v and actor observations. At control step 563 the independent replay/contact calculation predicted target changes of +/-0.0138372 rad, and the two physical hip drives changed by +/-0.0140108 rad, within the preregistered 0.001 rad tolerance. The control path was therefore active.

The positive variant first terminated at step 678; the negative variant at 697; and the matched no-feedback pulse control at 693. Neither recovered from the induced pitch excursion and remained within 0.01 rad of the undisturbed trajectory for 50 consecutive steps. Both feet had positive net upward branch impulse until termination, while the opposed toe-normal proxy was present in 627/678 and 652/697 intervals respectively. That proxy does not establish grip, and these four extra intervals are not evidence of a robust improvement.

The signal is the approximate displacement between source-mass-weighted COM and final-substep vertical-impulse-weighted contact x, without velocity feedback; it is not a full 3D contact wrench or validated biological sensor. The pulse is internal joint actuation, not an external push. This is scripted diagnostic feedback on a development fixture, not a neural controller, continuously landed mission or held-out qualification. Complete source, binaries, commands, hashes, both full traces and failures are archived.

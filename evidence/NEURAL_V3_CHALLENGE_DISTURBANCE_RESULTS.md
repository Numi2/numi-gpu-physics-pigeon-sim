# V3 challenging starts and matched perturbations

The two previously consumed hard development starts failed the 1000-interval test: seed 20310201 first terminated at interval 596 after a longest qualifying hold of 478 intervals (41–518); seed 20310221 first terminated at 563 after a longest hold of 446 (40–485). For comparison, v1 first terminated at 298 and 190. Automatic reset after failure is present in the files but excluded from the results. These starts were already used and are not held out.

For both signs of a prescribed idealized ±0.04 m/s horizontal root-velocity intervention at episode tick 2600, the live v3 policy survived all 500 intervals and qualified continuously from 38 through 500. Live and frozen controls for each sign matched for their first 200 complete recorded frames. Against the unperturbed run, the first 200 frames also matched and the velocity and other state diverged at the intervention. The accepted root x velocity in the first post-intervention recorded state was +0.02054 m/s for the positive condition and +0.00435 m/s for the negative condition, versus -0.00055 m/s without intervention; contact and dynamics act before that sample, so these are not direct measurements of injected impulse.

The plus frozen-output control also qualified from 38 through 500; the minus frozen-output control had a 433-interval streak from 38 through 470, remained unterminated through 500, and violated the tilt gate during the tail. Thus this pair shows changing residuals affect the tested response for the negative condition, while the positive control shows the network is not necessary to pass that case. Neither result isolates sensory feedback from time-dependent output changes, demonstrates broad perturbation robustness, or qualifies autonomous flight and landing. These are controlled, relocated-branch development fixtures with a scripted carrier.

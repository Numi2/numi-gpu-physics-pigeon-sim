# Isolated v15 visual and solver checkpoint prototype

The experimental, task-specific host can save the resident solver, contact/manifold state, task and action history, direct masked-depth visual history, cached renderer buffers and sensor phase, then restore them into a fresh context. It is not merged or a general production checkpoint. It starts from the existing reconstructed, relocated branch fixture with a forced landing phase, approximate action history and scripted carrier. It cannot recreate the original 48-second run or prove autonomous landing or balanced perching.

The first comparison was invalid because the uninterrupted path performed a warm-up that checkpoint-enabled paths skip. In a matched comparison, the prefix then reproduced exactly but the first resumed physical step diverged: its network observation and action matched, but the wing-drive continuation target was zero and the wing velocity differed by about 12.93 rad/s. The traced cause was the resident effort-trajectory buffer, which is reused across submissions but was not included in the existing retinal-oriented checkpoint. The isolated second build includes that buffer.

With matching checkpoint-enabled runs, the updated prototype reproduced every recorded q, v, body state, actor observation, accepted action, reward, outcome and termination flag across these tests: seed 20370127 split after step 50 through step 100; seed 20370113 split after 49 through 100; seed 20370127 split after 51 through 100; and seed 20370113 split after 200 through 240. These are exact equality checks on recorded values, not evidence of long-duration robustness. On the first resumed step of the step-50 test, all 128 logged scene contacts and all 16 leg effort samples, 16 wing effort samples, drive targets, phase and feedback samples matched the uninterrupted run.

Modified and truncated checkpoint files were rejected; a different seed and a changed camera field of view were rejected by contract checks. Commands, complete emitted traces, failed and corrected comparisons, checksums, checkpoint files, experimental source, build records and binaries are archived in the accompanying verified bundle. No unsupported broader checkpoint qualification is claimed: multi-lane resets, other camera paths, policy changes, later failures, operating-system/device changes and complete user mission remain untested.

# COM signal diagnosis: articulated inverse mass was unavailable

The preregistered 620-step positive-pulse diagnostic reproduced every control q/v frame, actor observation, action, body state and termination. The diagnostic reward intentionally differed in 70 frames to carry telemetry; no training used this run. From step 551 onward, repeated telemetry returned zero total mass, branch body 40 and nonzero branch vertical impulse. At step 562 the GPU reported 0.00160190160 N s, agreeing with the independently parsed retained final-substep contact trace to better than 1e-9 N s. The calculated balance signal and subsequent action-path snapshot were zero.

Thus the inverse-mass lookup used in the initial observer supplies no bird body mass in this articulated runtime representation. It is not evidence that the v15 model has zero mass: a separately built observer using the exact model construction reports 0.45006 kg across 35 bodies. The first feedback trials remain invalid for judging controller effectiveness. The next diagnostic binds the observer to those verified model-specific masses and independently audits its signal. It is still a research fixture, not a neural or autonomous landing result.

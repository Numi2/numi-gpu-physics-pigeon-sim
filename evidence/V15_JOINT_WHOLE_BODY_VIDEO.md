# Joint whole-body policy, five views

This montage contains the old separate 48-second flight replay, the four-update joint whole-body policy on hard development seed 20370113 through its first fall at 5.18 s, and an earlier hold by a different policy. Front, back, above, below and hip/feet views are synchronized within each segment. The historical flight had collisions disabled. The perching fixture has a relocated reconstructed branch, forced phase and scripted carrier; this is not a continuous autonomous landing. Ground is visually hidden in the below camera.

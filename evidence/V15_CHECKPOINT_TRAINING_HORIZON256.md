# Failure-inclusive checkpoint training trial

A 256-step, single-lane PPO update began at the verified reconstructed checkpoint using the same source policy and optimizer as the 128-step trial. It recorded one failure at rollout step 132 (terminal reward -2.1298). Gamma was 0.999 and lambda 0.995; the terminal TD error's GAE coefficient at the first step was 0.455. The first-episode GAE and discounted Monte Carlo advantages had correlation 0.996, while at termination the critic value was -3.154 and the measured return -2.130, giving a positive terminal advantage of 1.024. This is one stochastic sample and does not prove that the critic caused the failure; the positive advantage reflects return relative to that estimate. Actor and critic gradient clipping were separate, and protected actor weights, biases, normalization and policy contract were checked.

The resulting candidate and original policy were compared from the same saved physical state on both consumed starts. Both first terminated at relative step 151 on seed 20370127 and relative step 136 on seed 20370113, with reason 2. No reliable improvement was observed. Full rollout, optimizer, policy, learner logs, attribution computation, source, and paired replays are retained.

The subsequent reset investigation found that these checkpoint starts differed in their first physical step from ordinary warm-up starts because the experimental wing-drive target history survived warm-up/reset and the reconstructed start falsely appeared to be an already active episode. These results remain valid for the recorded states, but they do not qualify a reset-independent policy or autonomous landing. The corrected reset is a separate later experiment.

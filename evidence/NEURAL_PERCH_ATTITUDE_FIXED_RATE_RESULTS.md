# Matched fixed-rate attitude residual update

The fixed 0.0003 and 0.00003 learning-rate updates consumed byte-identical rollouts with identical decoded fields and fingerprints (SHA-256 `05c339a78b40af1bd77fe6e1f035b11be785a842c9374e251de3a40b27f94710`). The higher-rate update preserved all protected actor parameters and contract values. On the same retained observations, its maximum per-action RMS target change was 0.000153 rad for the leg outputs and 0.000295 rad for the toe outputs; toe targets are gated and those values are not a direct measure of applied motion.

The post-final-step balance KL summed across eight outputs was 0.006934; separate per-output values are in the native training log. Actor and critic gradient norms were each separately clipped. The training rollout recorded eight done events including resets. These diagnostics establish a larger update under the matched conditions, not that it improves balance. On development seed 20370113 it fell at interval 266 (5.32 s) versus 253 for the lower-rate update. On seed 20370127 it fell at 399 (7.98 s) versus 356. Both are failures and the earlier direct-contact policy held the second start through 1000; the small paired differences on consumed starts do not establish robust improvement.

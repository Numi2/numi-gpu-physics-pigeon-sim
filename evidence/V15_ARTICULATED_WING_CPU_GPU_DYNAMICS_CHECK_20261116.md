# FP64 reference comparison for wing torque response

The v3 bounded zero-gravity fold/hold/unfold/hold trajectory completed 1920 uninterrupted solver steps, with four wing joints near their target and nearly still at both holds. This is not an independent dynamics accuracy check.

At the unchanged default pose and zero velocity, compare one 0.0001 s free-motion GPU step under +0.001 N m left-elbow effort against the production-independent FP64 articulated forward-dynamics reference at the identical q/v, zero gravity and no body damping. All other generalized efforts and external wrenches are zero; task, policy, contact and aerodynamics are absent. Record all CPU and GPU accelerations, solver diagnostics, binary/shader/source hashes and FP64 total linear/angular momentum before and after the GPU step. Predict both solvers accept, the infinity norm of their acceleration difference is at most 0.005 times the larger acceleration infinity norm plus 0.01 rad/s², and each component of the GPU state momentum change is below 1e-7 in SI units. Preserve a mismatch as a failure; matching at one state does not establish contact accuracy, energy conservation over long runs or calibrated anatomy.

# Body-motion plus contact training on two starts

After exact zero-output simulator parity, four PPO updates alternated a physically reached step-150 state on consumed hard seed 20370113 and a step-50 state on consumed seed 20370127. Each 256-step exploratory rollout included a fall, at steps 101, 155, 103 and 162 respectively; reset data after each fall is not continuous balance. The first update used a fresh optimizer. The initial attempt to resume it with the fresh-only worker was correctly rejected; the matching optimizer's protected moments were verified zero, and a continuation worker was used with protected-weight, bias, normalizer, log-standard-deviation and optimizer-moment checks after every subsequent update. All checks passed.

The learned path responds to body-motion inputs on the retained observations: subtracting the zero-residual baseline in each arm, zeroing the first nine inputs changed the eight joint-target differences by up to 0.000813 rad RMS. This numerical ablation does not establish that the response is physically useful. On matched deterministic evaluation from earlier checkpoints, baseline and candidate both fell at absolute step 257 on the hard start; neither fell within the 250 resumed intervals on the other start, with peak tilts 0.12947 and 0.12958 rad respectively. The candidate is not promoted.

All model and optimizer states, sampled rollouts, stopped attempt, continuation guards, numerical ablation, exact commands and simulator traces are in the split `crow-v15-attitude-balanced-training-20261115.tar.gz` archive with per-file hashes. This remains a relocated, reconstructed branch, forced-phase and scripted-carrier experiment on consumed development starts, not an autonomous landing or generalization test.

# Reset-fixed higher-rate diagnostic

The same stochastic rollout from the same step-49 reconstructed checkpoint and policy was used to test an actor learning rate of 0.0009 versus 0.00003. The initial attempt to restore the old optimizer was rejected because the learning-rate contract differed; that failure is retained. The successful diagnostic started a fresh optimizer, so the result does not isolate learning rate from optimizer reset. An intermediate launcher error occurred before training and is recorded separately.

Protected actor parameters, all biases, normalizer and log standard deviation remained equal to the baseline. On the retained observations, eight leg/toe target changes had RMS values from 0.000095 to 0.000964 radians, compared with approximately 0.000001 to 0.000017 for the lower-rate continuation. Same-checkpoint, first-observation-matched evaluation on development seed 20370113 fell at step 258 versus baseline 257, a single 20 ms step. On seed 20370127 neither fell in the 250-step comparison, but candidate maximum tilt increased from 0.129 to 0.226 rad. The candidate is not promoted; neither comparison establishes robust balance.

Its per-file hashes are in the accompanying manifest. The same archive includes the exact-match first observation check for the earlier reset-fixed training run.

The hard result nevertheless provides a reproducible diagnostic equilibrium for investigating recovery.

The pulse is a controlled internal joint actuation, not an external push or a neural action; it must not change root forces, limits, friction, branch geometry or checkpoint state. Build a separate shader-bound checkpoint for each arm and verify its pre-intervention q/v and drive targets match the 1500-step unperturbed arm. An insufficient excursion is inconclusive, not a pass. Both signs must pass before considering external pushes, disturbed starts, learning or autonomous landings.

# Contact-gated physical wing lowering: failed balance screen

Both shader-bound checkpoints exactly matched all 49 baseline prefix frames. In the already-used hard-start branch fixture, the first flap target change was interval 53 and both q/v streams were identical to the matched fixed-hip control beforehand. The -0.4-radian trial reached physical flap angles of about -0.0535 radians and first terminated at interval 454; the -0.8-radian trial reached about -0.4534 radians and first terminated at interval 377. The unchanged control terminated at interval 625. Both candidates therefore failed the preregistered 500-interval balance gate. Their flap angles returned near +0.349 radians at termination as the angle-based lowering gate faded out.

For the two first episodes, both feet recorded net upward branch impulse at every interval through termination, while the front/rear toe-normal opposition proxy persisted for only 406/454 and 330/377 intervals, respectively. Opposed normals are not independently validated grasp stability. The wing target changes were physical position-drive diagnostics under a fixed hip policy, not neural control. The branch is reconstructed, the start is already consumed, and this is not an autonomous arrival, landing, or held-out layout.

Rendering also overstates wing deployment in these fixtures: the presentation fold schedule requires both navigation completion and recorded ground support, but this fixture does not report navigation completion. The detailed feathers are estimated geometry; the v15 body has no independently actuated elbow or wrist capable of a full crow-like fold. Do not promote either target or treat a presentation-only fold as proof of a physical fix.

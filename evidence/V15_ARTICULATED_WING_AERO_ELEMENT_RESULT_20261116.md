# Six-link quasi-steady aerodynamic load experiment

The isolated pre-dynamics Metal callback applied loads to bodies 5, 35, 37, 6, 38 and 40 of the v3 articulated fixture. The wind-off and wind-on arms each accepted all three declared single-step lanes. In still air with a motionless body, all six loads were exactly zero. Under mirrored extended-wing airflow, left and right forces matched in x/z and were opposite in y; the bent moving wings changed forearm and hand forces. The maximum per-component GPU/FP64 force difference was 4.1e-8 N and torque difference 2.7e-10 N m. Matched off/on generalized-velocity differences reached 0.00792 and 0.00629 in the two moving-air lanes, confirming the generated wrenches reached the dynamics solver. Exact inputs, code, binary/shader hashes, build logs, raw per-link loads and states are retained under `crow-v15-wing-continuation-20261116/wing-aero-element-20261116/`.

This passes the one-step numerical criteria, not physical or biological aerodynamic qualification. The quasi-steady closure and area distribution are uncalibrated; it omits wake, morphing-surface occlusion, feather deformation, unsteady lift, fuselage and tail drag. The test does not run the old actor, powered flight, contact, landing, or navigation. The experiment is a dedicated callback harness, not yet integrated into the task's validated compiled aero program. The prior non-penetration and controller limitations remain.

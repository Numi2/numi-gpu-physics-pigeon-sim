First-rollout SHA-256 exactly matched the previous v3 update-01 (`8aa91a5d8c2d5ac4c05041cf658664d5432a0e86889e1766e7f52e3589fc34b6`). All protected-parameter and optimizer-moment guards passed. Final per-action KL and separate actor/critic norms are in the logs. This demonstrates an executed joint update, not a successful physical policy.

From the same hard development checkpoint (seed 20370113), the joint actor first terminated at absolute step 259 (5.18 s), versus zero baseline step 257 (5.14 s), a two-interval difference. It missed the preregistered ten-interval criterion. On the easy development seed 20370127, it had no termination in the 400-interval (8-second) continuation, with maximum tilt 0.12988 rad in the first 250 intervals. First actor observations matched the recorded comparator on each start. The candidate is not promoted.

On the hard run both feet had branch contact and positive net upward impulse in all 210 intervals through first termination; the opposing front/rear normal-x proxy held for 158 consecutive intervals. On the easy run those values were 400/400. Opposing impulse is a geometric proxy and does not validate a stable grasp. Logs retain all 16 substeps, and post-done frames are excluded from these results. The body/control feature weights changed across all 18 action rows on retained observations; there is no demonstration that this movement was useful.

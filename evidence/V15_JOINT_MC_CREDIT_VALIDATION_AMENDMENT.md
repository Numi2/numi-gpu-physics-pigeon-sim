# Monte Carlo credit validation amendment

The first `v15-joint-mc-update-01` attempt computed an independent Monte Carlo reference before the run and emitted worker credit telemetry, but did not enforce their equality inside the worker before the optimizer update as the plan required. It exited before an attempted termination could reach the already-finished trainer. Treat this entire first attempt as invalid for the preregistered test, regardless of its outcome; retain its source, all outputs, rollout, policy, optimizer and failed termination observation. No physical evaluation or policy promotion will use it.

Amend the isolated worker to compare the independent reference sample count and first, last, mean and standard deviation to its independently calculated credit, with absolute tolerance 2e-6, and throw before the learner update on any mismatch. Run one new, separately named attempt with unchanged initial policy, native checkpoint, seeds, rollout length, settings and experimental prediction. Check the native rollout SHA-256 against the original joint update-01 and preserve the invalid first attempt alongside the corrected run. Physical comparisons and stopping criteria remain exactly those in the original plan.

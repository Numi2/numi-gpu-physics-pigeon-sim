# Test joint-driven reactions to developing forward tilt

The fixed -0.75 hip-output actor held the reused hard checkpoint for 500 steps but crossed 0.35 rad at step 541 and terminated at step 625; the first 500 states exactly reproduced the shorter run. Its easy-start peak tilt exceeded 0.15 rad. Four PPO updates on the wing, tail, knee, neck and hip-roll residuals varied their outputs only slightly and did not meet the easy gate. The next question is whether a bounded, timely response to *developing* pitch can reverse the existing failure, before asking the neural actor to learn that response.

Using the identical physically reached hard step-49 checkpoint, fixed -0.75 policy, v3 body, actuators, relocated branch, native executable and no teacher sampling, run four research-only shader arms for 750 intervals (15 s): positive and negative feedback on both hip position targets, and positive and negative feedback on both wing-flap position targets. Enable feedback only during the supported perch phase and when accepted body pitch exceeds +0.20 rad. The bounded diagnostic target offset is sign times clamp(0.30*(pitch-0.20)+0.10*max(pitch_rate,0), 0, 0.04) rad; this uses accepted-state pitch and body pitch rate, applies through existing joint drives, and adds no root force or constraint. These state signals are a privileged diagnostic teacher, not the deployed neural actor. The early gate should keep the easy-start baseline unaffected, subject to testing.

Before interpreting any arm, confirm from the shader's exact task-state lane assignments and recorded q/v and drive targets that the feedback uses pitch and pitch rate as intended, that initial state and pre-activation frames reproduce the original v3 baseline, that the applied target changes have the expected sign and bound, and that the simulation contains no resets. A mismatch invalidates the arm. Preserve all four full traces. For advancement require first-episode support and opposing-toe proxies on both feet, no termination or tilt >=0.35 rad over all 750 intervals, plus an actual return from at least 0.24 rad to below 0.20 rad sustained for at least 50 consecutive intervals after feedback activation. Compare against the retained fixed -0.75 control. If any arm advances, separately preregister matched easy-start and explicit, opposite-signed perturbation tests before considering feedback distillation into the neural network. All trials use already-consumed development starts and a forced perch with scripted carrier, so cannot qualify an autonomous landing or held-out layout.

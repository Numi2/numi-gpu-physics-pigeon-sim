# Toe sensor continuation: training

Four fixed-rate sensor-only continuation updates completed. For every update the inherited actor weights, biases, normalization and exploration parameters matched the reference exactly. The recorded training rollouts had 8, 8, 8 and 9 done events respectively (including later automatic-reset episodes); this is not a first-episode qualification or evidence of improved balance. The actor pre-clip norms were approximately 0.0154, 0.0138, 0.0137 and 0.0125 with separate clipping. All rollout packs, checkpoints, optimizer states, commands, logs, code and hashes are preserved in the accompanying Git archives.

The final checkpoint fell at interval 253 (5.06 seconds) on development seed 20370113 and at interval 345 (6.90 seconds) on seed 20370127. Its starting separately clipped policy fell at intervals 264 and 295 respectively; the earlier long-horizon policy fell at 266 and had no fall through 1000 on the second. The final checkpoint fails both trials and does not recover that 20-second hold. All first-episode traces and summaries are archived. Every intermediate and earlier checkpoint remains available; no policy is promoted on training metrics.

On the retained identical observations, the maximum per-action RMS leg target change was 0.00000249 radians and the maximum per-action RMS toe target change was 0.000000189 radians relative to the zero-connected source; the gated toe values are upper bounds. These are target comparisons, not evidence of successful physical control.

# First policy update from a saved contact state

A research-only trainer restores an exact one-lane checkpoint after 50 intervals of the reconstructed branch fixture, validates the source deployment policy, and then installs the compatible dense learner policy. Its first actor observation equals the original resumed observation exactly. The training-shader checkpoint reproduced the evaluation-shader q, v, observations, actions, body states and termination flags for its initial 50 intervals. The corrected trainer build and first failed build, source, binaries, snapshot, commands, logs and complete 128-step physical rollout are retained.

The 128-step update used gamma 0.999, lambda 0.995 and learning rate 0.00003. All protected policy weights, biases, normalization, exploration and contract fields stayed unchanged; intended residual weights changed. The rollout contained no termination, whereas the source policy fell 151 intervals after this checkpoint in deterministic evaluation. Thus this update did not actually observe that failure; a longer-horizon trial is required before deciding whether failure attribution improves learning.

The policy-fork mechanism was checked: restoring the same snapshot and reinstalling the same policy exactly reproduced all recorded outcomes for 50 steps. At both consumed development starts, the new candidate saw the same first observation as the baseline. They fell at the same first interval: 151 after the seed-20370127 checkpoint, and 136 after the seed-20370113 checkpoint, both reason 2. Automatic post-fall resets do not count toward balancing. The candidate is rejected, and these reconstructed, relocated branch trials with a forced phase and scripted carrier do not establish an autonomous landing or robust perch balance. Their startup differs from the earlier warm-up path that produced a 20-second partial demonstration.

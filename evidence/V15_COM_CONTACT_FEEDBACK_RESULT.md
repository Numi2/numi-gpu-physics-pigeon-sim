# COM/contact feedback trial: invalid instrumentation

Two physical hip-feedback signs were tested after the same internal hip-position pulse, starting from shader-specific checkpoints whose 49-frame prefixes exactly matched the control in q, v, observations, actions, body states, reward and termination outcomes. Both 1,000-step runs reproduced the positive-pulse-only control exactly across the entire recorded q, v, observations, accepted actions, body states, reward and done fields; the first terminal step was 693 in all three. Neither feedback variant changed any logged drive target. These runs **do not test the effectiveness of the feedback** and do not demonstrate recovery.

An independent reconstruction from the control shows that the last-substep net vertical branch impulse at step 561 was negative, so the planned positive-support gate should indeed reject that sample. At step 562, it was +0.0016019 N s and the reconstructed mass-weighted COM/contact/rate signal was approximately -0.0011644 m, predicting an unclipped target correction of about -0.000699 rad at the following step for the positive variant; none appeared. The expected alignment and target saturation remain to be independently diagnosed in the subsequent instrumented trial. Impulse-weighted position is a vertical-impulse approximation, not a full wrench center or a grip/slip measurement. The disturbance is internally commanded hip motion, not an external push.

The automated analysis contains a zero maximum pitch difference after activation when no target difference exists; this is a default for an empty interval, **not a recovery result**. Retained source, compiler commands, checkpoints, complete traces, and failed runs are archived with SHA-256 manifests. No neural policy was trained or promoted.

# Perch center-of-mass and contact-impulse observer

The retained v15 model construction was rebuilt with the matching linked runtime library and the two-segment wing-inertia header. Its 35 bird-body names exactly matched the recorded replay; all reconstructed body-local COM offsets were zero and the mass sum was 0.45006 kg. The model's printed wing joint/drive metadata exactly matched the disturbed rollout. The host source and runtime-library SHA-256 values also match the previously archived exact-checkpoint prototype manifest. This supports using the replay's accepted body positions with the reconstructed mass weights; it does not validate the biological mass distribution.

In the unperturbed hard-start run, the bird COM's world-x offset from the vertical-impulse-weighted foot/toe contact point was about 0.0236 m at interval 550 and 0.0240 m at 680. Under the positive hip pulse, it changed from 0.0236 m to 0.1516 m over those intervals; the total upward contact impulse remained present. With wing-release suppression, the latter offset was 0.1429 m. This isolates a growing whole-body displacement relative to the support impulse during the fall. The impulse-weighted point is an approximation, not a full contact-wrench center, a support polygon, or evidence that the toes slipped. No biological validity or causal torque conclusion is implied.

The mass observer was corrected after two recorded setup failures: an incorrect header path and an earlier incompatible wing-model header. Its final source, header, binary, hashes, build log, metadata parity check, comparison script and both failure records are preserved in the adjacent archive. Raw replay and contact inputs remain in their respective previously archived manifests.

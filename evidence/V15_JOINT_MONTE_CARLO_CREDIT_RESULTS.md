# Matched Monte Carlo actor-credit experiment

The first attempt did not enforce the independent reference comparison before updating and was declared invalid before evaluating its policy; its worker and all outputs are retained. A separately named corrected attempt enforced first/last/mean/std and sample-count equality before the update, and passed. Its native rollout was byte-identical to the matched GAE joint update (`8aa91a5d8c2d5ac4c05041cf658664d5432a0e86889e1766e7f52e3589fc34b6`); behavior-log-probability error was 0.0000153 and protected actor/optimizer guards passed. The alternative changed the actor's first-episode credit to normalized full discounted return-to-go while leaving the critic GAE target, fixed learning rate, sampled experience and all other declared settings unchanged.

On the same retained episode, the terminal reward-to-go was -2.0773 and the across-episode mean was -2.8676 with standard deviation 0.3658. Thus, even without a critic baseline, the terminal decision's normalized relative credit was positive. This does not establish what its policy-gradient contribution was in isolation, and does not prove the only cause of failure.

The matched one-update GAE and Monte Carlo policies each first terminated at absolute step 257 (5.14 s). Their initial actor observations matched and physical q trajectories diverged, so this is equal outcome rather than identical behavior. Both feet registered branch contact and net upward impulse through all 208 first-episode intervals; each had 156 intervals of the opposing front/rear normal-x proxy, which is not a validated grip criterion. The Monte Carlo actor did not reach the 10-interval preregistered improvement, is not promoted, and the conditional easy-start test was not run.

The experiment uses a previously consumed hard development start with a reconstructed, relocated branch, forced phase and scripted carrier. It is not an independent layout, continuous flight, autonomous landing or biological validation. Full source for both worker attempts, independent reference and failing validation history, all policy packs, optimizer states, rollout data, commands, action and contact traces and exact hashes are retained in the accompanying archive.

# Matched leg-posture direction screen

Four static bilateral output-bias probes were run from the identical reconstructed hard checkpoint, with the same initial actor observations and all other actor outputs frozen. The retained zero control first terminated at relative interval 208 (absolute 257, 5.14 s). Hip +0.5, hip -0.5, ankle +0.5 and ankle -0.5 first terminated at relative intervals 161, 275, 195 and 195 respectively (absolute 210, 324, 244 and 244). The applied first hip and ankle target deltas were respectively +/-0.04 rad on both sides, within existing joint limits; all trajectories and native targets are in the archive.

The hip -0.5 arm had positive upward branch impulse on both feet throughout its 275-interval first episode, and a 226-interval longest bilateral opposed-front/rear-normal proxy streak versus the zero control's 156. It first crossed 0.35 rad tilt at relative interval 194 versus 131 in the control. Thus this arm passed the preregistered exploratory screen and delayed excessive tilt and termination by 63 and 67 intervals; it still fell, and the opposed-normal metric alone does not prove stable grasp. The other three arms worsened first termination and are not promoted. A separate matched easy-start trial was preregistered before being run.

The start was an already-consumed development seed, with a reconstructed and relocated branch, forced perching phase and scripted carrier. The correction is a fixed diagnostic neural-output bias, not learned whole-body recovery, autonomous landing, generalization or biological validation. The source, all packs, all post-failure data, physical target/contact logs and hashes are retained. Baseline target data were extracted from the independently verified earlier zero-control archive and are supplied as `V15_LEG_POSTURE_ZERO_TARGETS.json` with its source hash.

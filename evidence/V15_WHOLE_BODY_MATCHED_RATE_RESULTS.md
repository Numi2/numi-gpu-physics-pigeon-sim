# Whole-body matched fixed-rate comparison

The preregistered comparison uses one PPO update from the same initial policy, seed, physical training checkpoint, learner seed and fresh optimizer, with only the fixed learning rate changed from 0.0003 to 0.003. The two native rollout packs are byte-identical: SHA-256 `8aa91a5d8c2d5ac4c05041cf658664d5432a0e86889e1766e7f52e3589fc34b6`. Original actor outputs 0–7 are identical on the same retained observations, while new output RMS movement across channels 8–17 ranges 0.00118–0.00234 at the low rate and 0.00550–0.01924 at the high rate. Actual bounded joint-target shifts are scaled by 0.08 or 0.10 rad per unit, subject to joint limits. Actor and critic clipping are separate. After the final update the total 18-channel KL on the training sample was 0.001117 at the low rate and 0.055111 at the high rate; per-channel values and actor/critic gradient norms are in the retained logs. The high rate is not an adaptive-rate test.

The policies were forked from the same physical checkpoint for each development start; first actor observations agree. The first replay frame's q/v is recorded after the differing actions, so q/v are not expected to match there. On hard seed 20370113 the low-rate actor first terminated at absolute step 256 (5.12 s) and high-rate actor at step 253 (5.06 s). On easy seed 20370127 neither first episode terminated through 400 resumed 0.02-second intervals; first-250-interval maximum tilt was 0.12940 rad low and 0.13298 rad high. The high-rate update fails the preregistered criterion and is rejected.

Both feet registered branch contact and net upward impulse at every pre-termination interval of the hard trials and all 400 easy intervals. Opposing front/rear x-normal impulse occurred at both feet for 155/207 hard-low and 151/204 hard-high intervals, versus 400/400 on each easy trial. This proxy is not a validated measure of stable grip. All scene substeps and scripts are archived; counts exclude post-done intervals. The test demonstrates that a larger fixed update can move this actor but does not improve this failure; it does not isolate the ultimate cause or establish that reward attribution is sufficient.

The seeds are consumed development starts on a relocated branch with forced perching phase and scripted carrier. They are neither autonomous landings nor unused-layout qualification. Retain both failed and apparently successful outcomes, source, manifests, optimizer and policies. Reconstructed archive parts and each source file were hash checked.

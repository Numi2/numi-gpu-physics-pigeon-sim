# Train whole-body residuals around the hip posture lead

The fixed -0.75 hip bias produced a 10-second hard-start hold in one development fixture but failed the preregistered easy-start tilt limit. A constant bias cannot be accepted as a controller. The existing v3 research actor can also command bounded wing flap, sweep and pronation, tail, knee, neck and hip-roll channels through physical joints. Prior PPO updates without the hip offset moved those outputs but did not solve the hard-start failure. Test whether learning coordinated, state-dependent residuals can improve the failed easy-start criterion without losing the hard-start benefit.

Predeclare four one-environment PPO updates, alternating the retained hard seed 20370113 step-150 and easy seed 20370127 step-50 physically reached development checkpoints. Start from the retained v3 stochastic actor with a -0.75 shift in both hip output biases; preserve its action distributions, observation normalization and all other model parameters. Use the existing v3 training shader, learner, reward, 256-step horizon, two epochs, gamma 0.999, lambda 0.995 and fixed learning rate 0.0003 from the previous update series. Train only the existing 42-feature whole-body residual weights for outputs 8 through 17; protect the original actor and hip bias, and record source, policy, shader, checkpoint and learner hashes, all rollouts, optimizer states, per-update actor change and first failure. Do not treat a training rollout or increased KL as success.

After all four updates, evaluate the deterministic deployed actor against the already retained fixed -0.75 actor on matched physically reached hard step-49 and easy step-50 checkpoints, 500 and 400 consecutive intervals respectively, teacher off and no resets. Require hard: no termination, tilt below 0.35 rad and bilateral upward impulse plus opposed-normal proxy at both feet throughout; require easy: no termination, first-250 peak tilt <=0.15 rad and both contact proxies throughout. Record first-done, peak tilt, source and actor-output parity where applicable, every joint action, and 16-substep contacts. Check that learned outputs vary with observation; fixed bias alone does not satisfy whole-body learning. If the candidate misses either gate it fails. These are development experiments on the reconstructed branch, with a scripted carrier and forced perch phase; neither constitutes held-out testing, autonomous landing, proof of grasp, or biological validation.

# Prediction contradicted: damped supports drift

The experimental fixed-world bias was applied with the existing predicted-position integration, then removed from the active research checkout. At damping 0, maximum support-position error was 2.38e-9 m, maximum free-node y force residual 7.41e-5 N, and final-to-initial total energy ratio 0.99834. At damping 100, support error was 1.3052e-6 m, exceeding the preregistered 1e-8 m bound by about 130 times; the force residual was 6.49e-5 N and energy ratio 0.83673. Both stationary and incoming +/-0.01 m/s controls returned successful native status, but status alone is not physical qualification. The linear equality certificate did not detect final position drift.

Source inspection after the trial shows the world constraint compiler turns positionError into damped finite-time stabilization, not an exact positional correction; this is a candidate explanation and requires a new, independently checked formulation. The full patch, native build output, all eight raw run files and stderr are retained here. The candidate uses the unchanged 0.001 s timestep; it did not qualify nonlinear coupling, moving targets, mixed contact, feathers, flight or landing.

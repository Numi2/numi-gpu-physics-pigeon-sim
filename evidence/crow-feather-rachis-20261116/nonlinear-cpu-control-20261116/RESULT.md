# CPU fixed-support control: prediction contradicted

The CPU native solver reported success after 76 sweeps in both damping cases, but the maximum free-node configuration-based y-force residual was 0.805850183 N, far above the preregistered 0.01 N bound and worse than the GPU candidate's 0.010320275 N. Elastic energy rose from 7.86246505e-6 J to 8.73754483e-6 J even in the undamped zero-gravity case. For damping 100, velocities are exponentially damped after the projection and the documented configuration-force check deliberately uses displacement rather than post-decay velocity. This is an unexpectedly poor control, not evidence that the candidate passes or that the solvers are comparable in all details. It needs diagnosis, including energy-gradient alignment, solver convergence, and the test model; no physical or flight claim follows.

The executable source and raw output, compiler identity, and source/library SHA-256 hashes are retained here. Compiled on macmini with Apple clang++ -O3 -std=c++23 -arch arm64, include=repo/include, linked to build/wing-capacity-48/lib/libmetalrobo.dylib; invoked once per listed damping value with the same 1 ms timestep and 4096 max sweeps.

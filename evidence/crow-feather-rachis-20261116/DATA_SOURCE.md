# Carrion crow feather rachis measurements

Lees J, Garner T, Cooper G, Nudds RL. 2017. “Rachis morphology cannot accurately predict the mechanical performance of primary feathers in extant (and therefore fossil) feathered flyers.” Royal Society Open Science 4:160927. https://doi.org/10.1098/rsos.160927

Original data: https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.4616380.v1 ; downloadable original `rsos160927_si_001.csv` from https://ndownloader.figshare.com/files/7506184 . Authors and publisher license: CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Original publisher MD5 `e84f5ba66852a87c6c862206371acaad` matches the saved exact bytes. SHA-256 `4e92157bacdb7feb26c5bc21e19ac9e34d20f3c26dd70879942553a7390f55a8`.

The paper identifies 78 carrion crow (*Corvus corone*) primary-feather samples across individuals; these are related-species measurements, not American crow samples. The CSV retains all 180 raw samples and original missing entries. `c1` left `P5` was selected for instrument construction, and all other birds remain reserved for possible later analyses. No complete-feather bending curve or individual American-crow measurement is contained in this provenance record.

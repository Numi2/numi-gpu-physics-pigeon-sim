# First exact-bilateral world trial

Both previously rejected damping cases returned success with the experimental exact Schur path, while the stationary control stayed essentially at rest. That is not physical qualification: the undamped bent rod's elastic energy increased from 7.862465e-6 J to 9.689708e-6 J (23.2%), and its largest support-position error was approximately 1.72 micrometres. The damping-100 arm also increased elastic energy to 8.102243e-6 J. Detailed retained states, certificates, energy and support errors are in the JSONL and analysis files. A solver success status is not treated as evidence that integration is correct.

The world adapter forms attachment stabilization from the unconstrained predicted rod position, while constrained integration later updates from the free trajectory and a velocity correction. An earlier archived start-state experiment is relevant, but used the old regularized solver and did not enforce the equalities. The next matched test must jointly check position, velocity, kinetic plus elastic energy, and independent force balance at the same timestep, including moving-target and free-rod controls. No articulated hand, contact, aerodynamic or mission claim follows from this trial.

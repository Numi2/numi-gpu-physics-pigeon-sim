# All-frame three-axis force audit

Both retained damping-consistent candidate trajectories fail the predeclared 0.01 N free-coordinate force screen. Across 1,800 free-coordinate samples per arm, the undamped maximum absolute residual was 0.01251818 N at step 2, node 2, x; the damped maximum was 0.01204204 N at step 94, node 5, x. At the respective maxima, central-difference force estimates spanning 3e-9 to 3e-10 m differed by 2.55e-12 N and 2.80e-11 N, well within the 0.001 N instrument-resolution bound. Both simulations previously completed the support and energy screens, but those screens did not expose these x-axis failures.

The evaluated residual uses the specified backward-difference physical screen, which is not claimed to be the split solver's exact discrete equation. This observation does not itself identify whether spatial quantization, model linearization, integration, or another effect is the cause. It rules out promoting the experimental candidate under the declared screen and does not qualify the world, feather, articulation, flight or landing. Per-frame force samples, all residuals and source identities are retained; no timestep was changed and no simulation was rerun.

# Planar nonlinear implicit control, preregistered

Construct a research-only independent planar implicit-Euler control for the identical nine-node, zero-gravity, undamped 1 ms fixed-support state. Lock x/y on support nodes 0, 4, 8, allow x/y of all other nodes, and keep z and twist zero. Minimize the native CPU elastic energy plus the inertial displacement energy. Compute force residual directly by centered differences of the authoritative native energy; use two gradient steps 1e-9 m and 3e-10 m and report all coordinates, objective, residual and energy. Before viewing results: require both runs to attain <= 0.01 N maximum free-coordinate force residual, differ by <= 1e-8 m at all free coordinates, and leave total (elastic plus kinetic) energy no greater than the initial energy. Compare to the native CPU-supported result. This frozen planar surrogate is a diagnostic, not a production solver or an articulated, three-dimensional, contact, aerodynamic or flight qualification. Preserve raw optimizer warnings and failure if criteria do not pass. No timestep reduction.

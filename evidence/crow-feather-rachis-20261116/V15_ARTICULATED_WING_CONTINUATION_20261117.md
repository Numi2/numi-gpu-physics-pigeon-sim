The live obstruction is architectural, not a timestep setting. `HeterogeneousRodProgram::rigidBindings` in `include/metalrobo/HeterogeneousWorld.hpp` index non-articulated scene bodies; `HeterogeneousWorld::valid` rejects non-scene indices, and `compileMetalWorld` maps those bindings through `sceneBodyIndices`. Authored-body endpoint seeding and generalized row solve in `src/metal/MetalWorldContact.metal` likewise use free-body response/application for `BODY_LOCAL_POINT`; articulated manifold contacts use separate dynamic point-query/Jacobian plumbing. A live feather cannot currently be connected to the articulated crow hand through this API. Do not work around that with a kinematic proxy or a fake free body.

Use the actual current v15 point Jacobian and generalized inverse mass, including dynamic joints, to compile and apply a two-way articulated rod attachment. Couple hard equalities with the contact space solve, preserving their residual after contact updates and ensuring failure rolls back transactionally. The archived 73-coordinate CPU and Metal references (`articulated-kkt/`, `articulated-metal-kkt/`) provide fixed-pose one-step numerical targets; `mixed-contact/` gives one synthetic frictionless active/inactive normal target. They do not provide nonlinear, aerodynamic, multi-contact or performance qualification.

The nonlinear reference previously showed that a linear trajectory can have a native nonlinear force residual of 0.0345 N despite matching energy closely; exact-linear-mode/split experiments are not a replacement for nonlinear dynamics. Avoid reducing timestep as a solver fix. Establish physical vane/shaft mechanics, twist and aerodynamic coupling, folded clearances, and real motion against converged references at the production stepping policy. Then requalify disturbed flight and freeze the controller, qualify articulated landing and recovery, and execute all waypoints continuously with supported balance on both genuinely unused layouts/seeds. Render a new full multi-angle video from actual successful native states, keeping failures and traces in Git. Existing videos are not evidence of that result.

# Translation-dependent force control

The translation predictions held in all four 10-step comparisons, and all steps reported successful status. At damping 0, maximum force residuals were 0.012518 N at the original location, 0.011973 N when centered on zero, and 0.291535 N when translated +1 m. At damping 100 they were 0.012042 N, 0.011974 N and 0.291536 N, respectively. The worst force estimates remained resolved under the three finite-difference steps. The centered results are still above the unchanged 0.01 N criterion.

Thus the simulated/observed mechanics have strong absolute-position sensitivity under this FP32 path, and a reported successful step does not certify force balance. This is consistent with spatial precision being a major contributor at +1 m, but the experiment does not isolate every source of the remaining centered residual or reconstruct unrounded hidden positions. No altered or centered setup is promoted as a fix. A translation-robust representation and integration must be checked with the same unchanged timestep, force, energy, attachment, momentum and rollback requirements before physical feather integration can be qualified. Full states, force samples, statuses, source, build, hashes and residuals are retained.

Step 100 residuals are also retained, but are not a full nonlinear or all-frame force audit.

The candidate alters a global world correction and is only instrumented here for stationary supports with no CCD, one rod, no contacts and no moving targets. Its scaling of velocity correction also needs work-conjugacy and contact review. The patch is preserved as an experiment and is not a qualified production fix or evidence of flight, landing, physical feathers, or articulated coupling.

# Experimental front/rear toe sensing

The actor currently receives aggregate bilateral foot support but no separate front/rear toe contact or toe-joint proprioception. The existing compiler resolves joint position and velocity only through action bindings, while the experimental physical toe drives are shader-side residuals rather than individual task action bindings. This plan starts with separate front/rear accepted-contact wrench signals, which the task compiler already supports, rather than mislabeling action coordinates as toe joint observations.

Build a separate host library that adds left/right front/rear contact groups and appends their three local force components as actor-current observations, retaining the existing rear groups and underlying v15 physical shapes. Do not alter or replace the shared checkout's host, task, frozen flight controller, or existing evidence. Compile the changed task, identify its exact new fingerprint and observation layout, and migrate a copy of the prior actor with zero-weight input columns using the native PolicyPack extension path. Its original inputs, normalization, critic, weights and actions must remain equivalent; bind only to the actual new task contract. Verify insertion position and the full deterministic physical rollout against the matching old host on consumed development starts before training. If groups are not populated or parity fails, preserve the failure and do not train/promote the candidate.

Sensor forces are simulated accepted-contact wrenches, not validated biological measurements. The held-out 2036-series starts remain excluded from development. Any subsequent training must have its own preregistration, retained optimizer outputs and matched physical evaluations; no success may be inferred merely from changing observation dimensionality.

# Toe projection: physical trajectory and drive-target observer

On consumed development seed 20370113, the retained full and direct-toe-projection-ablated policies produced their first greater-than-1e-9-rad toe-coordinate difference at replay frame 39. On seed 20370127 it was frame 35. Later maximum toe-coordinate differences before first done were 0.0321 and 0.00924 rad respectively; these later differences include the coupled dynamics of already divergent trajectories. The summary outcome was unchanged in both cases, as recorded in `NEURAL_DIRECT_TOE_CAUSAL_RESULTS.md`.

The read-only target observer was separately linked with the toe-sensing host. On seed 20370113, both full and ablated observed runs exactly reproduced their corresponding original q, v, actor observations and accepted actions for all 100 replay frames. Their first toe-drive target difference greater than 1e-8 rad occurred at observer trace step 41, and the maximum difference in the 101 logged trace steps was 0.00010681 rad. The observer step counter is not assumed to be the replay-frame index. Target differences after divergence also include policy feedback. The results establish delayed effective target divergence, not whether the delay was specifically due to the bilateral support gate or joint-limit clamping.

The sensor aggregates toe contacts with any external surface, while the independently checked strict grip criterion requires toe contact on the perch. This remains a reconstructed forced-perch development test with a scripted carrier; it is not an uninterrupted landing or evidence of generalization.

# Six-link quasi-steady wing-load experiment

Question: can a native device-physics extension apply state-responsive aerodynamic wrenches to the actual upper, forearm and hand bodies, and agree with an independent reference? The pre-dynamics body position/velocity instrument passed the separately archived three-lane calibration. This is a prototype numerical model, not validated avian aerodynamics.

Use six separate rigid-link surfaces, four midpoint elements per segment, all attached to the exact v3 COM frames. Segment half-spans use the three current engineering lengths; total area per side is 0.075 m² (the old model's value) apportioned by segment length, an unmeasured prior. Each element's local center travels with its link: v_element = v_COM + omega × r. Inflow is wind minus v_element with the spanwise component removed. Use the explicit quasi-steady section closure CL=clamp(3*alpha,-1.2,1.2), CD=0.06+0.1*CL² and rho=1.225 kg/m³; angles, coefficients, and planar span distribution are assumptions rather than measured crow values. Lift is perpendicular to span and inflow, drag follows inflow; sum force and COM torque on each actual link, with no root-applied correction or artificial thrust. Surface interactions, wake, body and tail drag, feather compliance, flapping unsteady terms and self-occlusion are absent.

Run a three-lane, single 0.1 ms free-motion step with and without the extension, same q/v, zero gravity and zero joint efforts. Lane 0 is still air and zero q/v; lane 1 has extended wings in uniform (-5,0,-1) m/s wind; lane 2 uses the same wind with bilateral nonzero elbow/wrist angles and rates. Use the exact v3 source and native MetalWorld pre-dynamics callback and apply each link's wrench through the borrowed ABA body-wrench arena. Capture the actual wrenches before the solve, q/v input, and final state for both arms. An independent FP64 body-kinematics plus element calculation provides per-link force and torque reference. Preserve all inputs, source, compiler/binary/shader hashes, statuses and output.

Predict: all six native steps (three lanes times aero on/off) are accepted and finite; still-air force and torque are zero within 1e-8; uniform-wind extended-wing forces are bilaterally symmetric within 1e-5 N; all GPU versus FP64 force components agree within 2e-5 N and torque components within 2e-6 N m; the deformed lane changes at least one distal link force by 0.001 N relative to extended lane; wind-on versus wind-off changes the final generalized velocity by at least 1e-6 in each moving-air lane. A failure of any check is a failed instrument trial; no retry without a revised plan. An admitted force step cannot establish dynamic wing folding, reliable flight, a physical bird, or policy qualification.

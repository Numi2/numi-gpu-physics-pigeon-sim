# V3 longer-credit development comparison

Eight 4-environment × 512-step PPO updates completed with gamma .99, lambda .995, 16 substeps, a fixed learning rate, v2 contact reward and wider training resets. The first update used a fresh optimizer because the state-contract guard rejected restoring a .95-lambda optimizer into a .995-lambda run; later updates restored the new optimizer. Raw rollouts, policy packs, optimizer states, configuration, KL and actor/critic gradient diagnostics are retained per update.

The final deterministic policy was tested on the two *previously consumed* v1-comparison starts with the original narrow-reset shader and full-substep contact recording. Seed 20310111 had 363 consecutive qualifying intervals (38–400), no first-episode termination: the same discrete result as v1. Seed 20310129 had 360 intervals (41–400), no termination: v1 had 252 intervals (41–292) and terminated for tilt at 370. Both pass the development 400-step terminal-hold gate; this is not a new-seed qualification. Between steps 151–350 on seed 20310111, v1/v3 mean tilt was 0.12974/0.13303 rad and mean root speed was 0.000121/0.000523 m/s; on seed 20310129 those figures were 0.30351/0.12492 rad and 0.03894/0.000309 m/s. The first start therefore ties on the hold gate and does not clearly improve in these continuous metrics, while the second improves substantially.

The model remains a four-action neural residual over a scripted terminal carrier in a reconstructed checkpoint with a relocated branch and forced phase. No autonomous landing, unused layout, real-crow validity, 20-second hold, or general disturbance recovery is established by this result. The separate longer development tests address duration without consuming unseen seeds.

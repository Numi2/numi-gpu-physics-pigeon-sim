# v15 articulated-wing mechanics: retained results

Source candidate: `Numi2/numi-lab` experimental branch `agent/crow-articulated-wing-20261116`, commit `fd7509590`. Its capacity changes widen shared MetalWorld and Matter ABIs; they are not qualified for production. The original flight actuator is disabled on the fixture, and the old navigation policy was rejected by its native compatibility check. The long runs here are mechanics experiments with no task, learned policy, gravity, air forces, or feather rendering.

Preregistered plans are adjacent under `evidence/V15_ARTICULATED_WING_*20261116.md`. The result directories include commands, inputs and SHA-256 hashes, stdout/stderr, and generated state traces as applicable. `final-ctest.log` records four compact tests. No successful flight, biological validation, or whole-bird mission follows from these data.

- The initial 40-capacity run was rejected before dispatch because the modified tree exceeded its capacity. A full 48-capacity build initially failed a host/Matter layout assertion and succeeded after the matching experimental ABI changes. The existing 48-capacity navigation attempt was then rejected on outdated scene-body visual bindings. After explicit rebinding it was rejected because visual observations require a native policy; the nonvisual variant was rejected because this navigation task requires vision; the historical actor was rejected as incompatible. No checks were bypassed.
- Two preparation wrappers failed before launching the simulator: `crow-rebind-and-run-48-fixture.py` asserted a generated filename ending in `.hip-roll.mrvpack` even though the tool wrote `.wing-fixture.mrvpack`; `crow-rebind-and-run-48-fixture-retry.py` then requested nonexistent `--visual-config` instead of `--visual-observation-config`. The output assets and scripts are retained. These are wrapper failures, not physics runs.
- In the v1 free-motion torque control, both ten-step simulations were finite, but the contact solver exposed a rest regression: the one-microstep diagnostic found four initial manifolds versus none in unchanged v15, including 7.91 mm torso/wing penetration and forearm/hand overlap. This caused substantial motion even without gravity or effort.
- The v2 COM-frame fix removed torso overlap and zero-input motion. It retained two wrist speculative manifolds at positive 2 mm separation and zero impulse, so its preregistered zero-manifold prediction was false. The v2 25-pose fold sweep still found deep forearm/hand intersections.
- The v3 exploratory capsule proxies cleared 22 of 25 sampled bilateral poses at the preregistered 0.5 mm tolerance. Three poses remained in penetration; no self-collision pairs were filtered. The proxies have a deliberate 14 mm gap at the wrist and are uncalibrated. They do not represent feathers or establish biological realism.
- A continuous 480-step fold completed; a separate, model-fingerprint-matched static probe of its 480 recorded q states found no contacts. This discrete replay is not a continuous swept-contact or original contact-force check. A separate 1920-step fold/hold/unfold/hold completed without a solver failure. At the end of the two holds all four test angles were within 0.003 rad of target and speeds below 0.006 rad/s; contact statuses appeared in 851 control steps and were not individually attributed over that run.
- A one-state FP64/GPU free-motion comparison found max acceleration difference 1.78e-6 and linear/angular momentum changes on the order of 1e-13 or lower in SI units. This does not qualify contact, extended trajectories, mass distribution or aerodynamics.

The user-facing model, feathers, articulated aerodynamic force application, actuator calibration, joint-coupling data, safe limits, balance policy, flight, landing, unused-layout missions, and updated video remain outstanding.

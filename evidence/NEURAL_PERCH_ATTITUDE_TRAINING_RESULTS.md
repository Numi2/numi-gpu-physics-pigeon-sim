# Attitude-and-contact residual: first update

The fixed-rate, separately clipped update completed with every protected actor weight, bias, normalization value, exploration parameter and contract fingerprint unchanged. On retained observations, physical target changes from the initialized actor reached at most 0.000053 rad RMS among leg outputs and 0.000105 rad RMS among toe outputs; toe targets remain subject to a physical contact gate. Zeroing toe-contact inputs changed those outputs, which confirms contact-feature dependence on this dataset but does not establish causal success or isolate body-state effects.

The trained checkpoint, Adam state, rollout, logs and source are archived. First-episode development trials are underway. The zero-output parity and learned-input dependence are not proof of stability or a continuous landing.

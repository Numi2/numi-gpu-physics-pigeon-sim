# Tilt and grip timing

The retained strict-contact analyses were compared at the first tilt-limit failure after interval 100. On seed 20370113 the original, per-foot baseline, first-update and four-update trials first crossed 0.35 rad at intervals 186, 178, 176 and 178 respectively. On seed 20370127 the original did not cross the limit within 1000 intervals, while the three per-foot variants crossed at 216, 269 and 308. At each of those first crossings the independent all-substep evaluation still reported bilateral upward support and opposed toe normals on both feet. Speed crossed its 0.12 m/s limit later in each failed trial.

This supports examining body attitude control during established grip; it does not establish the ultimate cause of the fall, prove adequate friction, or rule out later loss of toe opposition. The exact interval data and analysis script are retained. Both seeds are consumed development cases on the reconstructed forced-perch fixture with a scripted carrier.

The first four-update v3 actor changes newly enabled position targets by sub-milliradian amounts on retained observations and delays the difficult-start first fall by two 0.02-second intervals in an authority-on/off ablation, well below the preregistered success threshold. For each of the four exploratory first episodes, GAE and Monte Carlo advantages correlate 0.9968–0.9991, but the terminal failure advantage is positive because the critic predicted a more negative value than the terminal reward. These observations do not establish whether weak credit or update magnitude is the principal limitation.

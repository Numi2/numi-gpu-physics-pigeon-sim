# Reactive tilt screen: sensor mapping correction

The first hip-positive run was invalid: although its first accepted observation matched the control, its hip drive targets changed by +0.04 rad on resumed interval 2 while the prior recorded body tilt was about 0.159 rad, below the specified 0.20-rad trigger. It terminated at interval 120. A second arm was interrupted; its partial trace and the earlier shader/checkpoint identity rejection are retained. No physical recovery conclusion is drawn from these attempts.

In the hierarchical-navigation perching path, the task update assigns `state.threatGeometry = float4(-pos.z, rootLinearVelocity.z, pitch, baseAngular.y)`: `.z` is pitch and `.w` is the body pitch rate. The first diagnostic accidentally used `.w` as pitch and `state.threatTeacher.x` as pitch rate. Before rerunning the preregistered four-arm screen, rebuild each otherwise identical shader using `state.threatGeometry.z` for pitch and `.w` for pitch rate. Recreate each shader-bound step-49 checkpoint and require full prefix parity. As already required in the original plan, verify exact preactivation state/drive-target parity against the retained control, trigger timing against recorded q/v and target sign and 0.04-rad limit; a mismatch invalidates the arm. Retain every failed attempt. The diagnosis is based on the mode-specific shader assignment and the observed early target change; it does not assert how other task modes use these fields.

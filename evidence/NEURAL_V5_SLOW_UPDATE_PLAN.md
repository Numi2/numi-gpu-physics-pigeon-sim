# V5: fixed small PPO updates on the phase-corrected development fixture

V3 held 20 seconds on two consumed arrival starts but fell on two other consumed starts, and V4 with the original 3e-4 fixed learning rate regressed on three of four. Retained v2 failure advantages motivated gamma .99 and lambda .995 for v3; this pilot keeps those values. On the phase-corrected settled fixture, first stochastic episodes at -3.5 balance log standard deviation currently last 142-149 intervals; this limits attribution and means the curriculum is not qualified. A single diagnostic update at 3e-4 produced final combined balance KL 0.0347. The critic norm was much smaller than the actor norm in that update, and actor clipped norm was 0.953. Test whether four deliberately smaller, fixed updates can improve actual behavior despite the short episodes; this is not an adaptive-KL experiment and a small KL is not the success criterion.

Start from v3 actor/critic weights with the already verified -3.5 first-four log standard deviations and a fresh optimizer, leaving all other noise and the physical phase-corrected narrow-reset shader unchanged. Four training updates use seeds 20270611-20270614, four environments, 512 intervals, two 512-row minibatch epochs, fixed learning rate 3e-5, gamma .99, lambda .995, existing physically grounded reward, signed disturbances at the same 100/300-interval offsets, all contact and tilt terminations, and no adaptive rate. Carry the resulting matching optimizer and policy from update to update. Archive every rollout, policy, deployment pack, optimizer, logs, source/build/input hashes, final-step per-action KL, behavior likelihood parity and actor/critic pre/post-clipping norms.

Evaluate the final candidate deterministically with the unchanged original v3 arrival-reset evaluation shader for 1000 intervals on the four already-consumed development seeds 20310111, 20310129, 20310201 and 20310221, with first-done and strict all-substep support/grip/tilt/speed measurements against archived v3. Include failed seeds and automatic resets only after identifying the first episode. Do not promote unless it improves the difficult starts without degrading the previously held starts and does not rely on termination artifacts. Any fresh-seed, layout, full-flight, branch-landing or biological qualification requires a separately registered test. Preserve failed or inconclusive outcomes.

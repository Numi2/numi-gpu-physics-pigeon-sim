# Small-amplitude pitch sensitivity controls

If the final v3 neural policy does not improve both consumed development starts, test whether the physical plant responds usefully to small live pitch corrections. Previous feedback sweeps used much larger joint-target corrections and failed; they do not determine the sign of milliradian corrections. Starting from the v1 neural policy, apply paired symmetric hip-target feedback after interval 50: +0.02 or -0.02 times (accepted-state pitch minus 0.13 rad), clamped to ±0.004 rad. Match all other physics, policy, contact geometry, branch, reset seed and 16-substep observation against the retained v1 controls on consumed seeds 20310111 and 20310129. Keep every result, including worsened capture or fall. The feedback reads a task-private accepted-state pitch field, so it is an experimental teacher/control only, not a deployable autonomous neural policy. The actor separately receives projected gravity and body angular velocity; if a correction is physically qualified on independent perturbations, its labels could later be distilled and checked on student-visited states. No result from this sensitivity control by itself proves learned balance, landing, flight or biology.

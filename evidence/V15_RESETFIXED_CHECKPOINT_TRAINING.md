# Reset-fixed v15 branch checkpoint and balance update

This is an isolated research prototype on a reconstructed terminal-phase start, relocated branch, scripted carrier, and already-used development seeds. It does not establish an autonomous flight-to-landing mission or a generally qualified perch policy. See `crow-v15-resetfixed-checkpoint-training-20261115.tar.gz.manifest.json` for all included paths and hashes.

The earlier ordinary warm-up and no-warm-up runs had identical first observations/actions but differed in maximum joint position by 0.4383 rad and velocity by 21.9759 rad/s at the first step. The reconstruction forces episode step 2400; its previous-target history slot was treated as initialized even at the first actual step. A diagnostic-only shader initializes both wing command slots at this forced first step. Warm-up and no-warm-up results then match on the measured first-step state and wing target/effort. Checkpointed and continuous runs match on tested segments; the first 100 frames on each of the two development seeds also reproduce the earlier warm-up trajectory. This is not a general reset fix or a recovered checkpoint for the historical 48-second flight.

From the fixed checkpoint at step 49 on seed 20370113, a 256-step PPO update with discount 0.999, GAE lambda 0.995, learning rate 0.00003, and the original optimizer captured one failure. All protected parameters, biases, normalizer and log standard deviation remained identical. The retained observations showed joint target changes of approximately 1e-6 to 1.7e-5 radians RMS. A descriptive comparison on the first stochastic episode gave GAE/Monte Carlo advantage correlation 0.993; the terminal error's GAE weight at the start was 0.416, and terminal reward was -2.120 versus a critic estimate of -2.396. This is one episode, not a causal attribution proof. The training rollout's first observation exactly matched the deterministic resumed checkpoint.

Matched evaluation starts had identical first observations. On seed 20370113, both original and updated policies terminated at absolute step 257, 208 steps after the checkpoint. On seed 20370127, both stayed upright during the 250-step comparison; this abbreviated result is not the earlier 20-second qualification attempt. The updated policy is not promoted. Source, scripts, exact checkpoint, failed controls, rollout, optimizer, policies, scene traces, commands and diagnostics are retained in the hash-verified archive.

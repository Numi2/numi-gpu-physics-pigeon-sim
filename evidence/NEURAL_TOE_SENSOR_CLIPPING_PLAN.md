The effective Adam parameter update need not scale in the same proportion, and the reward attribution analysis also exposed a possible credit problem. Test clipping separately rather than assume it explains behavior or use adaptive learning rate.

From the exact same normalized zero-connected starting pack, use the same physical shader, seed 20271111, one update, four environments, 512 intervals, gamma 0.999, lambda 0.995, two epochs, fresh optimizer and fixed rate 3e-5. In an isolated copy of the learner, clip the actor/log-std and critic gradient trees separately to the same norm limit; do not alter the loss, protected-actor mask or other training parameters. If rollout matching or protected invariance fails, preserve the failure and do not attribute differences to clipping.

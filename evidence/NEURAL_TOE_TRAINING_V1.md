# First eight-action neural balance update

One exploratory update started from the retained leg-only update-01 checkpoint with four newly initialized toe residual outputs, using 4 environments × 512 intervals, two PPO epochs, a fixed 0.00003 learning rate, gamma 0.99 and lambda 0.995. The teacher/scripted carrier remains active. Native training exited successfully, but that is not a balance result. The full stochastic rollout, deterministic and stochastic policy packs, optimizer state, commands, inputs and output are retained. The maximum native/learner behavior log-probability discrepancy was 0.0000172; final post-step summed KL across the eight controlled outputs was 0.00674. Mean actor and critic gradient norms before combined clipping were 9.96 and 3.44; their reported clipped norms were 0.938 and 0.336. Per-output KL and other training metrics are in the retained output. A separate four-start evaluation must determine whether behavior improved; the update is not promoted on these metrics.

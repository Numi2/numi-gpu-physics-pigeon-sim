# Matched reset-width diagnostic

Exploration controls on the same four v4 training starts showed the first-four-action low-noise policy still failed by interval 195; head/beak-only noise reduction yielded similar short survival to baseline. The training shader also widens the reconstructed reset neighborhood compared with the v3 deterministic development fixture: x/y span .012 m rather than .004 m and horizontal velocity span .10 m/s rather than .04 m/s. To test whether that reset width contributes, build a shader identical to v4, including reward, push schedule and contact physics, but restore those three narrower spans. On the same consumed seed 20270511 and four lanes, evaluate unchanged v3 baseline noise and low-balance noise with this shader, each collecting 512 stochastic transitions using a fresh learner optimizer. Compare first-episode contact/tilt failure and censoring to the already registered wide-reset results. This changes the physical initial states and therefore is a controlled curriculum-width comparison, not identical starts or proof of generalization. Keep every rollout and failed trial.

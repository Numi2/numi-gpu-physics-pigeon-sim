# Coupled fixed-support Schur prototype: partial, not qualified

The experimental GPU path solves nine fixed-world rod-node rows together using existing rod factor response columns and a small Cholesky system. It is explicitly limited to one nine-node rod and does not solve mixed contact or moving/articulated supports. Its one-step direction and rebound controls met their criteria; measured support velocity was below 0.02 mm/s in the loaded test. At 10 and 100 ms with one physics substep, maximum support error was 0.000429 and 0.000551 µm and midpoint force magnitude 0.62872 and 0.62870 N, close to the 0.62905 N reference.

The full preregistered outcome **failed**: at four substeps maximum free-node force was 0.01497 N (>0.01 N), and at eight it was 0.07156 N, midpoint force was 0.68020 N (>5% from reference), and left/right reactions were asymmetric (0.32736 versus 0.26832 N). Thus force convergence, symmetry, and the requested physical mechanics remain unqualified even though the fixed supports held. Status code 0 is not a force-balance certificate. The experimental patch, complete states, controls, input identities and source are retained; it is not promoted.

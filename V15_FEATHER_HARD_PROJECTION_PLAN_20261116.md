# Fixed-support hard-row projection diagnostic

The baseline's authored rod rows request zero compliance, but qualityNewton replaces that with a numerical floor. Test whether the existing unregularized generalized scalar response can remove the resulting equality error after the quality solve. In the experimental start-state/integration candidate, permit its existing post-contact generalized sweep to execute once after qualityNewton, without changing solver budgets. Restrict conclusions to this rod-only, fixed-world, no-contact fixture: this projection could invalidate quality contact complementarity and its existing certificates, and is not a proposed production coupling design.

Before execution, require the original incoming-velocity and displaced-at-rest controls to pass, loaded 10/100 ms supports and force/energy to meet their original thresholds, and the 4/8 substep convergence criteria to hold. Measure final state and physical residuals independently; quality certificates are pre-projection and must not be represented as post-projection certification. Archive failure and patch. Moving bodies, mixed contacts, CCD, rollback and cross-suite effects remain unqualified even if this narrow fixture passes.

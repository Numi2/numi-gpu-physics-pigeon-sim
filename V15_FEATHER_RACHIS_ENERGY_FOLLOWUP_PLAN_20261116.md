# Preregistered rachis-energy and pre-shaped reaction follow-up

The prior reaction test failed its analytic comparison and did not retain all node positions. Distinguish rod energy semantics from transient/reaction/termination effects without modifying the rod solver. Use the same selected carrion-crow c1 left P5 short-segment EI, axial area, diameter and unmeasured density as the failed test. The 25 mm support span and 25 um midpoint deflection are unchanged.

First, construct the analytical small-deflection simply-supported beam shape under a central point displacement, y(x) = delta*(3*x/L - 4*(x/L)^3) for x <= L/2 and its mirror on the other half. At 9, 17 and 33 nodes, evaluate native FP64 rod elastic energy at zero, plus/minus delta and at delta +/- 10 nm. Independently compute the centered energy derivative. Predict zero energy below 1e-12 J, sign symmetry within 1%, and energy derivative within 10% of 0.647488512 N at each resolution. This is a discretization and constitutive sanity check, not an independent specimen prediction: EI comes from the same measured E and I.

Second, using the nine-node pre-shaped state, zero initial velocities, and the same three hard attachments as the first experiment, execute exactly three sequential CPU-reference and Metal steps at h = 1 ms, zero gravity, damping 100/s, 4096 maximum sweeps and positional tolerance 1e-10 m. Use -25 um, zero and +25 um midpoint lanes. Save every node position, velocity, attachment reaction, solver status, full input and executable/shader identities. Predict all steps are accepted; final loaded reactions oppose displacement and lie within 15% of the analytical value, with CPU/GPU magnitudes within 5%; zero lane force < 0.001 N. An invalid, missing or rejected status fails that arm. The stricter correction tolerance is an instrument change, not proof of convergence or a production setting. Fixed small budget: three resolutions and three steps; preserve all failures and do not retry without a new plan.

The paper measures the base in three-point bending, not entire rachis flexibility or vane mechanics. This surrogate does not include feather taper, anisotropy, live wing attachment, airflow, self-contact, flight or landing and cannot qualify any of them.

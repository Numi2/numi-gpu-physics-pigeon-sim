# Related-crow rachis three-point instrument: failed

All three CPU conditions and the 500 batched Metal steps reported success. CPU and GPU reactions closely matched, and the zero and sign-reversal controls behaved symmetrically. The numerical result did not meet the preregistered beam comparison: the final midpoint reaction magnitude was approximately 0.00000944 N in both implementations, against 0.64749 N expected for the chosen uniform small-deflection beam, a difference of about 99.9985%.

This is an instrument failure, not evidence that the published feather is unusually soft or that the native code is biologically wrong. The solver often returned after one nonlinear sweep at the configured correction tolerance; the retained data do not yet establish whether early stopping, reaction accounting, dynamics, geometry, or a model-semantics error explains the discrepancy. Both numerical implementations sharing a result is not an independent physical reference.

Limitation discovered after execution: the saved history includes all midpoint positions, midpoint velocities and attachment reactions but not every rod node position. Therefore the original plan's full-position-history requirement was not fulfilled; treat this run as invalid for detailed deformation reconstruction, and retain it without rerunning or silently changing settings. The exact source, inputs, statuses, logs and result are in `evidence/crow-feather-rachis-20261116/feather-rachis-three-point-20261116/`; the raw reference data and plan are alongside it. No feather, flight or landing qualification is claimed.

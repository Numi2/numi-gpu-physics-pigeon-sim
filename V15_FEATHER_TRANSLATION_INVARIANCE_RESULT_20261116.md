# Native free-rod translation invariance: GPU energy failed

With identical 0.125 ms initial conditions at x offsets 0, +1 and -1 m, CPU relative positions agreed within 5e-8 µm and energies to printed precision. GPU relative positions agreed within 0.058 µm, but total energies were 1.13683 and 1.01818 times the zero-offset GPU result at +1 and -1 m, failing the preregistered 1% energy criterion. All runs reported success. The discrepancy might include float32 world-coordinate quantization, coordinate-dependent finite-difference scaling, or both; this test does not identify their individual contributions. Raw states and hashes are retained. The baseline itself is highly dissipative, so the energy ratio compares the small remaining one-step energy, not total biological feather energy.

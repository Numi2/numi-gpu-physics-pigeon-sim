# Test whether correction-only convergence masks force imbalance

The retained fixed-support Schur trial has a free-node force residual of 0.07156 N at eight substeps while the rod reports a final iteration correction just below its configured 1e-10 m tolerance. Hypothesis: the absolute position-correction stopping criterion permits a state with an unacceptably large physical force residual. This is a diagnostic of the convergence certificate, not a claim that changing the timestep repairs the solver.

On an isolated copy of the previously archived fixed-world Schur prototype, compare the identical 10 ms, eight-substep 9-node supported rod at correction tolerances 1e-10 m and 1e-12 m, with all other configuration identical and iteration cap 4096. Capture statuses, iterations, states, velocities and independently evaluated free-node forces. Prediction: if premature correction stopping is the dominant cause and arithmetic resolves the tighter correction, maximum free-node force will fall below 0.01 N, left/right reaction imbalance below 0.01 N, and midpoint force within 5% of 0.62905 N. If it cannot resolve the correction or reaches the cap, the diagnostic fails and must not be interpreted as a production fix. A pass still does not qualify mixed contacts, moving attachments, dynamics, or real feathers; a suitable dimensionally meaningful physical residual and validated convergence are needed.

# Unconstrained translational midpoint rod oracle

Extend the restricted midpoint oracle to all 27 centerline position coordinates with the same native potential and nodal masses; keep all eight material twists fixed at zero. Use the identical nine-node initial state, zero gravity and damping, and eight 0.125 ms steps. Unlike the planar oracle, x, y and z are free. Predict nonlinear convergence at every step, total elastic plus translational kinetic energy within 5% of initial, center y velocity changes sign, and each component of net linear momentum stays within 1e-9 kg m/s of zero. Compare the prior native CPU/GPU unconstrained trajectories, noting any twist differences. This is an FP64 research oracle, not a production GPU integrator; fixed twists, nonlinear finite-difference precision, phase/frequency accuracy, contact, coupling and biological validity remain outside this trial.

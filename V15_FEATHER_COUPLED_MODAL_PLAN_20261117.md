# Constrained two-way modal dynamics control

Combine the two successful references for a narrow 1D translational model: the nine-node planar linearized rod is attached at nodes 0, 4 and 8 to three moving point masses with exact equality constraints. Eliminate the three support coordinates analytically, adding each support mass to its connected rod-node mass, and evolve the resulting mass-normalized constant-Hessian modes on the M4 Pro GPU. Start with matched nonzero rod/support velocities. Record 80 steps at the same 0.125 ms step, including displacement, velocity and acceleration. Independently reconstruct support positions and equal/opposite forces and compare against FP64 constrained dynamics.

Preregister: max node-position error <1 micrometre against FP64, energy drift <1%, momentum error <1e-8 kg m/s, support equality error <1e-9 m and max force residual <0.01 N for every step; all values must be finite. Archive states, native source, device, commands, input identity and failures. The support model is point-mass translational only; the fixed linearization and analytical coordinate elimination do not implement general 3D articulated-body contacts, nonlinear feathers, wing aerodynamics or flight. No timestep reduction or benchmark claim is made.

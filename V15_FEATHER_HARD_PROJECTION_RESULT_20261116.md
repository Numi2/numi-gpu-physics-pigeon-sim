# Post-quality hard-row sweep: failed loaded check

The isolated diagnostic combined the previously recorded start-state/integration candidate with one existing generalized scalar sweep after qualityNewton. Unloaded incoming-velocity and displaced-at-rest controls met their stated thresholds. The loaded 10 ms and 100 ms fixtures failed: maximum fixed-target errors were 4.893 and 6.429 µm, with midpoint force magnitudes 0.536 and 0.507 N against 0.62905 N ±5%. Errors were asymmetric. At four substeps target error was 0.495 µm and midpoint force 0.616 N; at eight, 0.114 µm and 0.681 N, so force convergence failed. The original success statuses and quality certificates precede the extra projection and are not post-projection certificates. The experimental patch is rejected and archived; no mixed contact or moving support was qualified.

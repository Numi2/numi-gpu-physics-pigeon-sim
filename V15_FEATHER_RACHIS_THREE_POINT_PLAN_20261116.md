# Preregistered related-crow rachis solver instrument

Question: can the native discrete-elastic-rod implementation reproduce small three-point bending of a short rachis segment with a measured related-species cross-sectional stiffness, before it is coupled to the v15 wing? This is a mechanics-instrument test, not an independent biological validation: the expected stiffness is calculated from the same measured E and second moment used to parameterize the rod. It does not represent a whole feather or American-crow-specific measurements.

Data: Lees et al., Royal Society Open Science 4:160927 (2017), DOI 10.1098/rsos.160927; raw CC BY 4.0 dataset DOI 10.6084/m9.figshare.4616380.v1, original `rsos160927_si_001.csv`, SHA-256 `4e92157bacdb7feb26c5bc21e19ac9e34d20f3c26dd70879942553a7390f55a8`, supplied and computed MD5 `e84f5ba66852a87c6c862206371acaad`. The paper reports three-point bending over 25 mm of the calamus of 78 carrion-crow primary feathers. Use individual c1, left P5 only: E = 2.129 GPa, Imax = 3.960 mm^4, area = 3.449 mm^2, diameter = 3.7 mm. Their product EI = 0.00843084 N m^2; use that measured effective stiffness directly. The specimen is *Corvus corone*, not *Corvus brachyrhynchos*; whole-feather taper, anisotropy and vane data are absent. Retain all raw rows, do not synthesize missing lengths, and reserve the other individual birds for future follow-up.

Instrument: a straight 25 mm, nine-node DER rod, fixed supports at both end nodes and a prescribed midpoint displacement, with `bendStiffness` set to EI and axial stiffness E times measured cross-sectional area. The collision radius is the measured external diameter/2; density 1200 kg/m^3 and isotropic torsion are explicit unmeasured engineering priors. Zero gravity; fixed support positions and midpoint shifts -25 um, 0, +25 um; otherwise identical inputs. Run the CPU reference and Metal DER with timestep 1 ms, 256 iterations and 1e-7 m tolerance, linear/twist damping 100 s^-1, for 500 consecutive steps per condition. Retain complete reaction/position histories, all statuses, source and binary/shader identities. No change or retry after seeing the results without an amended plan.

Prediction: all CPU and Metal steps publish; midpoint reactions at the final state are finite, oppose displacement, and have magnitude within 10% of the Euler-Bernoulli reference 48 EI |delta| / L^3 = 0.64749 N, with zero-displacement force below 0.001 N. Final GPU-vs-CPU reaction magnitude differs by less than 5% and the two supports plus indenter balance within 0.02 N. Retain all failures. Stop after the three paired conditions; a pass only qualifies this small-deflection numerical instrument under its assumptions. No tapered feather, aerodynamic force, collision, attachment to an articulated wing, flight, landing or balance claim follows.

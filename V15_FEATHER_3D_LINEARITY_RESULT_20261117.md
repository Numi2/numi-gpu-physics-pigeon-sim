# 3D native rod energy comparison

The preregistered prediction was partly wrong. Along the single archived 3D displacement direction, the native nonlinear energy and straight-rest quadratic model differed by 0.00014%, 0.00999%, 0.248% and 0.985% at scales 0.1, 1, 5 and 10, respectively. The original-amplitude <5% prediction passed; the prediction that scale 5 or 10 would exceed 5% failed. The native energy, stretch and bend components, all displacement vectors, source, commands, hashes and independent comparison are retained under `evidence/crow-feather-rachis-20261116/linearity-3d/`.

This is energy-only for one deformation direction of the same short, fixed-twist surrogate whose Hessian was linearized. It does not establish force accuracy, arbitrary large deformations, contact, aerodynamic or biological validity. It does not make the sampled modal control a production feather solver.

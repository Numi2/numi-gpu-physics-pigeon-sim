# Standalone free-rod backend control

All eight native CPU and GPU steps succeeded; their maximum corresponding node position difference after one millisecond was 0.0444 µm, below the preregistered 1 µm limit. Neither backend's total energy exceeded the initial energy. However energy dropped from 7.8625 µJ to approximately 0.2060 µJ after one 0.125 ms step and to below 4e-12 J after eight steps. The configured exponential velocity damping of 100/s alone does not explain the first-step reduction. This control passes its narrow backend-agreement and non-increase criteria, but raises a separate question about numerical dissipation; it does not validate realistic feather oscillations or the supported coupled path. Exact state, velocities, twists, input hashes and source are retained.

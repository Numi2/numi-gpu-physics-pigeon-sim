# Pre-shaped rachis force balance: failed

Finite-difference forces at 1 nm and 0.1 nm perturbations agreed to better than 6e-11 N per node; the sum across nodes was within 4e-12 N of zero. The near-equilibrium prediction failed: at saved CPU step 2 the maximum free-node y-force magnitude was 0.8063 N, and the indenter-node elastic force was -1.2096 N. The separately reported reaction on the indenter at that step was -0.00005662 N. The state is not established as a physical equilibrium and the attachment reaction cannot be used as a reliable static load measurement in this test.

Recomputing elastic energy from the recorded positions with zero twist gave 7.86247 microjoules initially and 8.73754 microjoules after the first step, an 11.1% increase, despite fixed target positions, zero gravity and damping. The stored state recorded node positions and velocities but omitted twist and twist rates. The source uses a straight isotropic intrinsic rod with zero initial twist and no twist drive; nonetheless this reconstruction limitation must remain explicit. These results warrant a dedicated solver residual and energy audit before relying on this rod path for feather coupling. They do not identify whether numerical convergence, force gradients, reaction accounting or another implementation detail caused the discrepancy.

The selected input, analyzer source, executable/input hashes and all per-node forces at both perturbation scales are preserved under `evidence/crow-feather-rachis-20261116/feather-force-balance-20261116/` and adjacent source. The reference is a short uniform carrion-crow segment, not a complete feather or an American-crow validation.

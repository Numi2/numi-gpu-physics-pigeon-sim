# Constrained two-way linear modal dynamics

The preregistered 80-step control passed on the M4 Pro with finite states and nonzero matched initial support velocities. Maximum position error against FP64 was 1.18e-10 m, relative energy deviation 1.53e-5, total momentum error 1.48e-11 kg m/s, and independently evaluated dynamic force residual 3.23e-5 N. The reported support equality error is exactly zero **by coordinate elimination**: each support coordinate is the attached node coordinate, not a separately solved state. Support forces were reconstructed from their finite mass and GPU acceleration, with the opposite force applied to the rod in the residual. No timestep change was made. Exact model, initial conditions, sources, full states, device, hashes and result are retained under `evidence/crow-feather-rachis-20261116/coupled-modal/`.

The reference is an exact linear fixed-Hessian model of a short 1D translational surrogate coupled to three point masses. It does not solve general nonlinear 3D articulated-body equalities, simultaneous unilateral contact/friction, changing attachment frames, aerodynamic loading or physical feather geometry. It remains a standalone research diagnostic, not a MetalWorld implementation or a flight and landing qualification.

Sampling limitation: the 0.125 ms output interval samples at 8 kHz; most modeled non-rigid modes exceed its 4 kHz Nyquist frequency. The exact isolated linear transition can match sampled states without resolving motion between them. This test therefore does not validate continuous interaction, collision detection, visible flutter, or rapidly changing wing loads.

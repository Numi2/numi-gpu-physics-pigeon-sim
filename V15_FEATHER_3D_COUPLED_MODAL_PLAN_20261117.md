# Three-dimensional two-way linearized centerline control

Compute a 27-by-27 finite-difference translational Hessian of the native nine-node rod elastic energy about its straight rest state, with twist fixed at zero. Couple each of three nodes to an independent moving isotropic point mass by exact coordinate elimination. If the Hessian is symmetric and its negative modes are confined to numerical near-zero values, compute mass-normalized modes, upload them in FP32 and evolve nonzero 3D displacements and velocities for 80 native GPU steps of 0.125 ms. Compare every sampled state to FP64 linear-modal evolution, energy and 3D momentum; independently compute force residual with equal and opposite point-mass reactions. Validate exact coordinate-elimination equality by construction.

Preregister: Hessian relative asymmetry <1e-10, no negative eigenvalue with magnitude >1e-8 of the largest positive eigenvalue; maximum position error <1 micrometre, energy deviation <1%, 3D momentum error <1e-8 kg m/s, and maximum force residual <0.01 N. Record inputs, code, Hessian step, eigenvalues, complete outputs and failures. Treat zero modes below the same positive 1e-10 relative threshold as zero and report their magnitudes. This is a translational, constant-Hessian linearized segment with point masses, not twist, general rigid-body orientation, nonlinear feathers, contact, aerodynamics or flight. Correct sampled states do not validate intervening high-frequency interactions. Do not reduce the timestep.

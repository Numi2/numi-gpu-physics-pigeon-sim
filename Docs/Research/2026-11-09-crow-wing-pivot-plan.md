# Estimated elbow and wrist flex: trial plan

Candidate 5 still rotates the distal wing about the shoulder, even though its
function is named wrist flex. That distribution can make the hand move like a
single sheet. Hypothesis: two estimated attachment pivots sampled from the
same lofted reference surface, with smooth spanwise weights, will permit the
outer wing to bend relative to the arm during the recorded upstroke while
keeping the root and feather attachments continuous. The simulator has no
recorded crow elbow or wrist angles; these pivots and the flex strength are
visual hypotheses, not measurements or added physics.

Before acceptance, compare the same five candidate-5 views and landing window,
check mirrored poses, fixed root and zero-strength identity, and reject visible
holes, instability or worse motion. The candidate-5 binary is frozen for the
in-progress two-layout replay; do not substitute a new executable mid-render.

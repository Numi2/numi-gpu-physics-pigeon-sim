# GPU underwing covert length trial

The independent layer ablations attribute most regular rows in the retained
overhead view to the GPU underwing coverts. Trial a 0.68 scale on their
root-to-tip vector, retaining their count, width, attachment and renderer.
The scale is an unmeasured presentation hypothesis, not crow morphometry.
Compare the same accepted 100-frame overhead and underside stroke with the
unaltered candidate and look for loss of coverage or excessive patterning.
Do not adopt a change based only on a static view; retain failed results.
The complete replay capture remains on the frozen candidate 9 executable.

Result: rejected. In the overhead comparison, the shortened vanes became
smaller, disconnected-looking bright scales while the periodic row pattern
remained. The underside inspection did not justify that regression. Both
full-stroke clips, their audits and the patch are retained. The active source
has been restored; further work should address actual layer placement,
materials and overlap against crow-specific reference data rather than a
uniform length multiplier.

# Shorter covert projections: qualitative trial

The top-view flight inspection shows very prominent regular dorsal covert
rows. Try shortening non-trailing visible covert projections to 78 percent of
the current root-to-tip vector, leaving the topology, hand feathers and
underlying surface intact. This is a visual hypothesis, not an anatomical
measurement. Compare the same retained flight stroke from above, below, side,
front and rear, looking for silhouette regressions, slots and collisions.
Reject if it creates holes or fails to improve the real-crow comparison.
Keep it independently gated and disabled by default. The in-progress complete
replays use the unchanged candidate 9 executable, not this experiment.

Result: rejected. The candidate completed the retained overhead stroke with
Metal API validation, but the paired candidate-5 images at frames 0, 25, 50,
and 75 show no convincing improvement in the prominent feather rows. The
remaining views were not warranted after this failed first criterion. The
source patch and video are retained as negative evidence, and the experimental
change was removed from the active renderer source.

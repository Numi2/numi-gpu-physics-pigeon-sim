# Visual reference and limits

The current replay render is an estimated visual registration of accepted v15
states. It is not a motion capture or an anatomically validated model of an
American crow. Cornell describes the species as having broad, rounded wings,
fingered tips, a short rounded or square tail and rowing flight; its crow vs.
raven reference describes five broad visible wingtip fingers. Sources:
https://www.allaboutbirds.org/guide/American_Crow/id and
https://academy.allaboutbirds.org/crows-and-ravens/.

In candidate 5, the five-view contact sheets show overly regular, high-contrast
covert rows and an overly rigid relation between wing and feather surfaces.
The resulting appearance is improved over earlier trials but is not yet a
validated photographic match. Candidate 6 explores elbow and wrist flex but
its visual improvement was too subtle to establish acceptance. Candidate 9
improves a projected support-foot alignment; it does not add measured
perching forces or measured wing and feather deformation. The two complete
route replays were previously used during development and must not be called
new held-out layouts. A few renderer-section joins have a small visible
transient: the ten-frame history warm-up test failed to remove it.

A crow-specific calibrated dataset of poses across flapping and landing,
including independent views, is still required to quantify and close those
anatomical and kinematic gaps. These references establish visual targets but
do not supply that dataset. No downloaded reference photo is included.

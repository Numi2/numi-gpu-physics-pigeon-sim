# Underwing material trial

The overhead ablation isolates a highly repetitive bright pattern to the GPU
underwing layer, and a uniform shortening only made it look like disconnected
scales. Test a less blue, lower-sheen underwing material while preserving its
geometry. This temporary shader edit is not a biological material fit. Compare
the same overhead and underside wingbeat against candidate 5 under the same
lighting; reject loss of feather definition, excessive flatness, new artifacts,
or no convincing improvement. Candidate 9 complete videos are already rendered.

Result: rejected. The matched overhead and underside strokes do not show a
convincing improvement; the repeating pattern remains and some feather
boundaries are less readable. The two clips, audit records, temporary shader
diff and shader hash are retained. Restore the renderer source and rebuild its
resources so this unqualified shader is not left active.

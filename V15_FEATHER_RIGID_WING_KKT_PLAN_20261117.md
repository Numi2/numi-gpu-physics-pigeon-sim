# 3D rod-to-v15 wing-hand rigid reaction control

Use the native fixture's left wing-hand mass and diagonal inertia (engineering priors, not measured American-crow values) to construct an isolated linearized 3D rigid-body/rod problem: nine rod nodes (27 translational coordinates), a free six-DOF rigid hand, and three hard point-attachment rows at a small local lever arm. Linearize the hand anchor as translation plus infinitesimal rotation cross lever arm; solve the rod and hand together using a dense native Metal Schur prototype. Test displaced/zero-velocity and nonzero-velocity starts in both rod-node orders, keeping the same 0.125 ms interval. The rod Hessian comes from the archived native energy and the hand properties from the actual v15 fixture. The hand is free in this control, not connected to the v15 joints.

Preregister: all four factorizations succeed; max attachment error <1e-9 m; max independently evaluated force/torque-equation residual <0.01 in the corresponding SI units; each position/rotation coordinate differs from direct FP64 KKT by <1e-6 in its own units; and mapped node-order differences are <1e-6. Verify equal and opposite 3D reaction forces and moment-arm torques, plus the residual in total linear and angular momentum balance. Save v15 property extraction, model, code, inputs, outputs, hashes and failures. Passing would not establish nonlinear rotation, full articulated-body coupling, feathers, contact, aerodynamics, biological accuracy or flight; no change to the production solver or timestep is authorized by the test itself.

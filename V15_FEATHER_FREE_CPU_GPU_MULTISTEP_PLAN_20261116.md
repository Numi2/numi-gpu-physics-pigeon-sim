Publish every complete state and energy. Predict all steps succeed, max CPU/GPU corresponding node displacement <1 µm, and neither backend's total elastic plus kinetic energy exceeds its initial energy by more than 1e-10 J. This is a targeted control, not an attempt to fix the system by changing timestep. A pass does not validate the coupled attachment; a failure will distinguish a standalone numerical problem.

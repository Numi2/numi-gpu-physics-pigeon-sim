# World-coordinate rounding control

At +1 m, converting the initial coordinates to float32 before any dynamics increased native FP64 potential by 3.0731e-8 J; at zero offset it increased by 1.3102e-11 J. The difference at +1 m is about 106% of the independently measured 2.8882e-8 J final GPU energy difference, meeting the preregistered scale prediction. At -1 m the added initial energy was 5.4275e-9 J. This shows world-coordinate rounding alone is large enough to matter for this segment; the numerical comparison does not prove it is the only cause of the final energy difference, since rounding is measured on the initial state and other dynamics also differ. Local-coordinate storage/coupling requires separate implementation and validation. Full exact positions and source are retained.

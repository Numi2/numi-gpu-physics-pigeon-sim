# Undamped free-rod energy control

To distinguish prescribed damping from integration dissipation, repeat the identical eight-step standalone CPU/GPU free-rod test with both damping coefficients set to zero and all other inputs unchanged. Preserve complete states, twists and energy each step. A loss of more than 5% of total energy on the first 0.125 ms step refutes adequate energy conservation for this test; the 5% target is a numerical diagnostic, not a biological qualification. Independently require CPU/GPU corresponding positions within 1 µm. No support, collision, airflow or material damping is present. Exact energy conservation is not assumed for the discrete integrator; failure identifies a dynamics limitation at this operating point and must not be hidden by comparing only CPU and GPU.

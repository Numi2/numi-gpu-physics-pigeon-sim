Question: does the passive-air energy increase decrease when the free-motion ABA integration is refined? Repeat the exact failed three-lane, zero-gravity, zero-effort, no-contact experiment and retain its full trajectory at 4, 8, 16 and 32 microsteps per 1/240-second control step, for the same one-second horizon. Use the same experimental shader, surface properties, independent FP64 energy calculation, initial conditions and matched aerodynamic-off control. Preserve each result even if a lane or criterion fails.

Before the run: predict that the maximum positive control-step energy increment of the aerodynamic-on root-translation lane decreases monotonically as microsteps increase and is at most 0.0001 J at 32 microsteps, while all solver steps are accepted. The original four-microstep result must reproduce. If this fails, no claim of convergence or passivity is allowed. A pass would suggest a discretization component but would not prove physical accuracy, constraint correctness, or safe behavior at other states; the experiment deliberately leaves joints and contacts unconstrained and must not be interpreted as anatomical motion.

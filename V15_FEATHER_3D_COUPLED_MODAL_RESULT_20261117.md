# Three-dimensional coupled linear centerline control

The preregistered 80-step Metal reference passed on the M4 Pro. Its 27-by-27 translational Hessian, computed by finite differences of the native rod energy, was symmetric to stored precision; the largest negative eigenvalue magnitude was 7.45e-11 of the largest positive eigenvalue. Five near-zero rigid modes were set to zero. Across all recorded GPU states, maximum error against FP64 linear modal evolution was 1.76e-10 m, maximum relative energy deviation 1.22e-5, maximum total 3D momentum error 1.47e-11 kg m/s, and maximum independently reconstructed force residual 3.39e-5 N. The three supports followed the attached node positions exactly by coordinate elimination, and support forces were reconstructed from their masses and accelerations. The timestep was unchanged. Full source, native Hessian, inputs, output states, hashes, GPU device and commands are retained under `evidence/crow-feather-rachis-20261116/coupled-3d/`.

This is a small 3D translational linearization around a straight rod with three point masses and fixed twist. It does not implement a nonlinear or fully articulated feather, rotational support response, contact, aerodynamic loading, or the actual crow mission. The 8 kHz state output undersamples much of the modeled spectrum, so accurate sampled values do not validate intervening high-frequency contact or visible motion. The result is a research reference, not a production solver or a performance or biological qualification.

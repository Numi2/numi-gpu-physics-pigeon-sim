# Experimental equality gate: narrow safeguard passes

The candidate fails a quality solve when the final hard-equality certificate exceeds the configured feasibility tolerance. The 0 and 100/s damping loaded fixtures both returned failure, quality status 7, world status 11, zero accepted steps and one failed step; their published rod states retained the original positions and zero velocities. An initially straight stationary supported control completed one step with status 0 and unchanged state. The patch prevents these tested false-success publications, but does not reduce the actual constraint error or provide a physical feather. Broader non-crow regression and CCD, mixed moving-body, and production rollback tests remain unperformed; the patch is retained as an experimental artifact and is not promoted to the shared solver.

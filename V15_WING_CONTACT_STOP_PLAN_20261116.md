# Preregistered wing self-contact stop test

Question: can the existing temporal-cone self-contact prevent penetration when physical drives push toward configurations known to overlap? Starting from the same clear default pose, run three continuous 960-step lanes at 1/240 s and 32 microsteps, zero gravity, no aerodynamic callback, with the baseline uncalibrated position drives. Ramp the bilateral elbow and wrist targets smoothly over 360 steps and hold them for 600: lane 0 safe control (1.8, 1.575 rad), lane 1 known upper-wing/hand overlap target (1.8, 2.1), lane 2 known body/hand overlap target (2.4, 0.525). Other driven joints retain default targets. The targets are deliberately unsafe probes, not recommended anatomical postures.

Predict all 2,880 lane-steps succeed; the safe control ends within 0.01 rad of each target; both unsafe-target lanes produce contact and stay at least 0.02 rad away from one or more requested elbow or wrist targets per side. For all lanes, audit 480 evenly spaced output states with the existing contact query and require no sampled separation below -0.0005 m. Save all observations, statuses, audit outputs and failures. Endpoint sampling and the incomplete capsule/box geometry cannot establish swept or feather clearance; even a pass would not qualify flight, anatomy, landing, or balance.

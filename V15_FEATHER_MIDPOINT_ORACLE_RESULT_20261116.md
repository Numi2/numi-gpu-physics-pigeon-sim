# Restricted planar midpoint oracle: energy screen passed

An FP64 research oracle used the native rod potential and nodal masses while restricting x and z and solving the transverse midpoint equations. All eight nonlinear solves converged in two iterations, maximum residual below 8e-9 N. The minimum total-energy ratio was 0.9999180 of its initial value (7.862465 µJ), and the center velocity changed sign repeatedly. This passes the preregistered narrow energy and oscillation screen and differs from the strongly dissipative original free-rod output. The comparison is not equivalent in degrees of freedom: the oracle fixes x and z, while the native DER run permits 3D motion. The test does not establish frequency or phase accuracy, biological fidelity, a Metal implementation, coupling, contact, or feather/wing qualification. Source and complete recorded states are retained.

# Native GPU linearized modal dynamics control

Use the previously retained native-energy transverse Hessian and nodal masses for the same 9-node short corvid feather-segment surrogate. Precompute its mass-normalized FP64 modes, treating only the two tiny nominal rigid eigenvalues below the previously documented 1e-10 fraction of the highest mode as zero. Upload the modal basis and frequencies as FP32 and evolve the same initial displacement with an exact linear modal transition on the M4 Pro GPU for 80 successive steps of 0.125 ms. Compare every saved position and velocity to independently computed FP64 modal evolution, total energy and momentum, with the same Hessian and initial state. The timestep is not changed.

Preregister: over all 80 steps, max node-position error <1 micrometre, energy deviation <1% of initial energy, all states finite, and total linear momentum error <1e-8 kg m/s. Record modes, input/output, commands, code, hashes and failures. This tests a planar, linear, free, constant-Hessian surrogate; it does not establish nonlinear 3D feather behavior, contact, articulated attachment, aerodynamic loading, biological accuracy or real-time throughput.

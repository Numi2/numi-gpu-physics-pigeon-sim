# Preregistered free-rod passivity isolation

Question: is the energy rise seen with fixed supports also present in unconstrained rod dynamics? Reuse the selected carrion-crow c1 P5 uniform 25 mm segment with measured E and I and the same density and area priors. For three and nine nodes, initialize the same analytic small-deflection beam shape at +25 um midpoint deflection, zero velocities and twists; run one CPU and one Metal DER step independently. No attachments, gravity, contact, or other external forces. Use h=1 ms, damping 100/s, 4096 iterations, 1e-10 m tolerance and the same derivative settings as the previous diagnostic.

Measure full pre- and post-step elastic energy, kinetic energy including translational and material-frame components, complete positions, velocities, twists, twist rates, solver status and identities. Predict all four steps are accepted; each final total energy is no greater than its initial value plus 1e-10 J, and CPU/GPU position components agree within 1e-6 m. If the passivity inequality fails, do not call this physical or blame supports. If it passes, fixed-support/reaction integration or its measurement remains a possibility; the test does not identify the cause alone. This is a two-resolution numerical isolation, not a feather, flight, or biological qualification. Preserve every result and stop after the two declared conditions.

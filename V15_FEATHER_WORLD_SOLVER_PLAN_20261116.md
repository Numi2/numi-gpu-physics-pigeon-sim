# Preregistered MetalWorld restrained-rod comparison

Question: can the existing heterogeneous MetalWorld constraint path handle the same fixed-support rachis test better than the standalone DER projection path? Compose the current experimental v15 body and a nine-node, 25 mm related-crow rachis surrogate at the +25 um pre-shaped state. Bind its two endpoints and midpoint to fixed world targets; these are world fixtures, not attachments to the articulated bird. No gravity, aerodynamic callback, rod-tool collision, or external actuation. Execute one 1 ms microstep independently for temporal-cone and quality-Newton modes from identical initial states, with full accepted rod state and solver statuses recorded. Preserve the world fingerprint, compiler diagnostics, inputs, solver/binary/shader identities and any rejected runs.

Compute post-step full rod energy and kinetic energy with the same FP64 native oracle, and centered 1 nm elastic y-force derivatives on every node. Predict both modes compile, dispatch and publish successfully; all three attached nodes stay within 1e-6 m of their targets; post-step total energy does not exceed the initial energy by more than 1e-10 J; and maximum unanchored-node y force is below the initial value and below 0.01 N. Reject missing or rejected state as failure, not a partial pass. The test contains only two declared solver arms and one step; preserve failures without changing settings after results. Passing would qualify neither whole feathers, attachment to the bird, physiological stiffness nor flight, landing or balance.

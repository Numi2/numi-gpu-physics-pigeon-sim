# Articulated wing checkpoint, 2026-11-16

The isolated Numi Lab source branch `agent/crow-articulated-wing-20261116` is pushed through `6b0829fa2212c170b20d289de557289e7d953828`; the original shared checkout was not changed. This evidence branch contains preregistrations, source, inputs, hashes, exact observations and failed trials. The branch expands shared MetalWorld/Matter capacities and changes ABI; it is experimental and must not be merged before independent cross-consumer ABI and runtime verification.

The mechanics fixture has actual left and right upper wing, forearm, elbow and two wrist coordinates. It is uncalibrated and is not yet an integrated crow flight model. Its original rigid-wing flapping program is removed. The six-segment aerodynamic callback is still a fixture-only research probe, not a reusable owner-integrated flight implementation; it uses a simple quasi-steady, no-wake engineering approximation with no feather forces. One-step CPU/GPU checks and live pre-dynamics body-kinematics checks passed in their limited samples. The original four-microstep free-wing passive-air screen failed; the 32-microstep version passed that narrow screen but did not demonstrate trajectory convergence. The elevated microstep count is not performance-qualified.

Fold targets were reached in still air after an extended hold. The unchanged drives missed in both 5 and 10 m/s wind. A higher-gain sensitivity candidate met the endpoint threshold at 5 m/s but failed at 10 m/s, with substantial free-body drift; it was rejected, and no gain was promoted. The contact-stop test showed expected wing/hand and body/hand reactions and no sampled penetration beyond 0.5 mm, while failing a separate preregistered joint-distance prediction. Static endpoint queries and deliberately incomplete bone capsules cannot prove swept no-penetration or feather clearance. The 25-pose sweep still contains three penetrating poses.

The native compact fixture tests pass 4/4, but there is no verified feather rig, complete aerodynamic validation, qualified articulated-wing flight controller, or continuous autonomous flight-and-supported-landing result on either reserved layout. Existing v15 controller/checkpoint contracts do not admit the changed topology. The older five-angle 48-second diagnostic is a prior replay, with a reconstructed perch, forced terminal phase, scripted carrier and a later balance fall; it is not an uninterrupted autonomous landing. The new six-panel wing video is a schematic generated from recorded FK states and is not a realistic crow rendering.

Next investigations must address realistic colliding/deformable feather geometry and swept contact; integrated, independently checked unsteady aerodynamic forces; actuator/joint calibration and stable bounded control under loading; compatible policy and observation migration; and separately qualified flight, approach, toe grip and recovery on untouched layouts and seeds. All claims must remain tied to actual native states and preserved failures.

# Nonlinear validation of sampled 3D modal trajectory

Evaluate every retained 3D GPU modal sample against the native nonlinear rod energy with twist fixed at zero. Reconstruct each physical state as straight-rest position plus its recorded displacement. Compute native elastic energy, kinetic energy of the rod and attached point masses, and the native energy gradient by central differences in all 27 translational coordinates (1e-9 m perturbation). Compare the gradient plus effective-mass times recorded acceleration to zero, and compare total energy with the native energy and kinetic energy of the original input state. The retained GPU trajectory, input and model are unchanged.

Preregister: across all 80 samples, maximum per-coordinate nonlinear force residual <0.01 N and maximum relative nonlinear total-energy deviation <1%. Record all force and energy results, exact commands, binary/source/input hashes and failures. Passing would only support this fixed-twist trajectory at sampled times. It would not demonstrate nonlinear time integration, continuous collision, rotational wing response, general feather deformation, aerodynamics, biological accuracy or a mission. No timestep reduction is used.

# Alternating sweep fixed-support candidate

The preregistered candidate failed its full gate. Alternating the existing short-rod projection sweep direction on successive iterations reduced the mapped forward/reversed node-order displacement difference from 0.3187 to 0.02301 micrometres. The forward case's maximum free-node force fell from 0.07156 N to 0.005776 N; the reversed case reached 0.010359 N, still above the preregistered 0.01 N limit. Midpoint forces were -0.623843 and -0.627016 N; endpoint-force differences were 0.000954 and 0.000573 N. Both native runs completed. The prediction therefore fails despite the improvement.

This suggests ordered iteration contributes to the observed sensitivity but does not prove a general convergence repair. The correction-only stopping rule, numerical dynamics, simultaneous contact, moving attachments and whole-feather behavior remain unqualified. The candidate was not promoted; it used the earlier fixed-world-only Schur prototype with a temporary equality-guard bypass for this diagnostic. The committed source and safety guard were restored after the experiment. The candidate patch generator, commands, hashes and both full numerical states are retained under `evidence/crow-feather-rachis-20261116/alternating-sweep/`, with the node-order fixture and Schur patch retained in their respective prior evidence directories.
